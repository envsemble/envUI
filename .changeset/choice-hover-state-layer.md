An unchecked Checkbox and a Switch that is off answer the pointer: under it and while pressed, the box's edge darkens to the text color, `--env-color-fg-default`, and the box stays empty, as a Radio's ring does, so it doesn't read as a checked box that is unavailable; the switch's track deepens a step as before and its ring darkens to the muted text color, `--env-color-fg-muted`. Checked boxes and switches that are on keep their hover and pressed fills.
