An unchecked Checkbox and a Switch that is off answer the pointer: under it and while pressed, the box takes the neutral state layer, `--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`, and its `-hover` step, with its edge a step darker, as a Radio's ring does; the switch's track deepens a step as before and its ring darkens to the muted text color, `--env-color-fg-muted`. Checked boxes and switches that are on keep their hover and pressed fills.
