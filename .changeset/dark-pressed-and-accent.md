In dark, a pressed primary or accent button, and the checked controls on the primary fill, lighten a clear step past hover, as far as hover is from rest, where pressed and hover looked alike (`--env-color-bg-primary-emphasis-hover` and `-pressed`, `--env-color-bg-accent-emphasis-hover` and `-pressed`). The dark accent fill, `--env-color-bg-accent-emphasis`, and a solid accent badge, `--env-color-bg-accent-solid`, are a deeper purple, as weighty as the primary's soft blue rather than the brightest fill on the page.
