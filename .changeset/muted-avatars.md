Avatars without a picture take muted colors: each of the eight hues, `--env-color-avatar-bg` and its `teal`, `green`, `amber`, `red`, `violet`, `purple` and `slate` variants, is a calm, low-chroma fill at the same lightness as before, so the initials and the figure keep their contrast and a group's hues stay apart. Slate is now the neutral blue-gray.
