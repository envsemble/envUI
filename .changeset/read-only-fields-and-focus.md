A read-only field, a TextField, Textarea, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, TimePicker or MonthPicker, or a Field.Control, has a quieter outline that doesn't answer the pointer, so it no longer looks like a filled field waiting for changes; its value keeps full contrast and its label its place, and a read-only Select, Combobox or picker keeps its chevron or calendar, muted, so it still reads as a choice. A focused field draws its outline twice as thick in the accent, all the way round the floated label, without moving anything, as do the color picker's value fields.
