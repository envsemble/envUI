A field's floated label takes a control label's size, `--env-font-size-label`, rather than the small body size: 13 px at the compact scale rather than 12 px, so it reads as easily as the value, and 14 px, as before, at the comfortable scale. This applies to TextField, SearchField, Textarea, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, TimePicker and MonthPicker.
