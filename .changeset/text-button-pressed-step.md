A text button's press reads apart from its hover. A danger text button with a label darkens a step under the pointer and another while pressed, as the link blue does, from the new tokens `--env-color-fg-danger-hover` and `--env-color-fg-danger-pressed`, where it kept one red in every state; in a selected table row it keeps the danger color. In the light theme a pressed link or text button, `--env-color-fg-link-pressed`, is a step deeper than before, two from its hover. The ESLint plugin and the Stylelint config accept the new tokens.
