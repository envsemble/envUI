Toolbar: `Toolbar.Item` makes any button-like control one stop of the toolbar's arrow-key path, such as a Button with a visible label, `render={<Button startIcon={<IconShare />}>Share</Button>}`, or a Menu's trigger, `trigger={<Toolbar.Item render={<Button>Paragraph</Button>} />}`. `Toolbar.Input` does the same for a text input, such as a font size. See [Toolbar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/toolbar.md).
