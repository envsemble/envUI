The calendar of a DatePicker, and a Calendar, take the approved Frosted design: the month's name leads the header in semibold body text, the two month buttons side by side at its end at 0.8 of the md control height; the days of a week touch, each a row height wide, with a hair between the weeks, under 1.75 rem weekday names in the caption size; today is in the accent color, semibold, rather than ringed, and a day under the pointer takes the neutral fill, as a popup's rows do. A MonthPicker's year header and months follow. In the picker cards Clear is muted, the text color under the pointer, quieter than Today, Now or This month at the start.
