Beside a label of two lines, a label that wraps or one with a description, the box of a Checkbox, the track of a Switch and the ring of a Radio sit on the middle of the label's first line rather than across both lines; a one-line label keeps them where they were. The rows of a Menu, a Select and a Combobox with a description put their check mark, icon, end content and shortcut on the label's line too.
