An app scale: `Theme` and `ThemePreferenceProvider` take `scale`, `comfortable` (the default, today's sizes) or `compact`, about 0.8 of them with type one step smaller, for desktop productivity apps. Left out, a Theme takes the scale of the Theme around it. Theme writes it to `data-env-scale` on every boundary, nested ones and those around popups included, and `useTheme()` returns `{ theme, contrast, scale }`. `createThemeBootstrapScript({ scale: 'compact' })` puts the compact scale on the root before the first paint. Use one scale per app or window, and another only on a Theme around a whole region.

Every size is a token, with a value per scale: `--control-height-xs`, `-sm`, `-md` and `-lg`, `--row-height`, the row densities' `--row-padding-block-*`, `--control-gap`, `--font-size-caption` and `--font-size-label`, `--icon-size-sm`, `-md` and `-lg`, `--radius-large`, `--field-floating-height`, `--row-content-height` and a tree row's paddings, `--tree-row-padding-block-sm`, `-md` and `-lg`, are new, and the spacing, type, corner, top bar and title bar tokens take their compact values in the compact scale. Icons keep a 1 rem floor, and borders and focus rings keep their widths. The type roles `--type-h1`, `--type-h2`, `--type-body` and `--type-body-small` are tokens now, with a custom property for each part, such as `--type-body-font-size`.

Removed custom properties: `--control-height-small` and `--icon-button-size`, which `--control-height` replaces, `--control-height-compact`, which `--control-height-sm` replaces, and the `--scale` multiplier, which components no longer read. `--control-height` is the height of a control that sets no size; a container can set it to another control height for what it holds, as table cells do with `--control-height-sm`.

Under a coarse pointer every control grows to 2.75 rem in either scale: buttons and icon buttons, tall and wide, fields, Select, the rails of SegmentedControl and Tabs, ButtonGroup, Pagination and menubar triggers. The controls of a row still match, and a control's own box is its hit area, so a press always reaches the control it lands on, whatever the layout around it. Segments, tabs and the actions inside fields take presses across their control's height, within it. Rows, navigation items and table rows with an action grow to 2.75 rem too, and the top bar and the title bar to 3.25 rem.
