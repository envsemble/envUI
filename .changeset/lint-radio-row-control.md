`no-field-in-list-row` lets a Radio be a `List.Item`'s `control`, which the row names and describes now, as it does a Switch or a Checkbox; a Radio in a row's `end`, and a RadioGroup in either, are still reported. See the [rule](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/packages/eslint-plugin/README.md#no-field-in-list-row).
