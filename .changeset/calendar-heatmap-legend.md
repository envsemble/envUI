CalendarHeatmap: `CalendarHeatmapLegend` is the heatmap's legend of color steps on its own, for the application to place, such as beside the heading above a heatmap with `legend={false}`. Give it the heatmap's `levels` and `tone`; `lessLabel` and `moreLabel` work as on the heatmap. See [CalendarHeatmap](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/calendar-heatmap.md).
