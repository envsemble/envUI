In dark, primary, accent and danger primary buttons, and the checked checkboxes, radios and switches that take the primary fill, lighten on hover and press rather than deepen, so their dark label or mark gains contrast in every state instead of losing it; light still darkens. A danger primary, and a solid danger badge, take a deeper, redder fill in dark (`--env-color-bg-danger-emphasis`, `#dd7c74`), so Delete reads as danger beside the accent's lilac rather than as a second pastel pink.
