**DocumentTabs looks like an editor's tab strip.** The strip takes the default surface color across its container with a hairline along its bottom, and the active tab takes the content's background with no edge below it, so it joins the content under the strip; there is no underline and no rounded hover fill. Tabs are rectangles the strip's height, parted by hairlines; an inactive tab's text is muted and brightens under the pointer. Tabs shrink to 7.5 rem, ending their labels in an ellipsis, before the strip scrolls. The behavior is unchanged.
