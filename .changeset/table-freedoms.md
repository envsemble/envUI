Table: `Table.Foot` renders a tfoot for totals or a summary row, on the header's shaded band, keeping its roles while rows stack. Table.HeaderCell takes `end`, content beside the sort button and outside it, such as a column menu, a filter IconButton or a select-all Checkbox; the header's name stays its label. Table.Root takes `regionProps` for the scroll region, such as a ref for scrolling to a row, a style with a measured height, data attributes or listeners. See [Table](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/table.md).
