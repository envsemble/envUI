Scaffold, the invisible structure of an application's screen, after Material's: a top bar region, the navigation and the body. The document scrolls by default, as on any website, with the top bar and the bar sticking to the window's edges and the rail or drawer kept in view; `scroll="body"` scrolls the body alone under the top bar, for previews, embedded frames, title-bar windows and Panes whose panes scroll on their own. It shows one navigation form at a time by its own width, in CSS: the Bar at the bottom of a compact width, the Rail at the start of a medium one and the Drawer from expanded, from `navigation={{ bar, rail, drawer }}`, and `forms` changes which form each window class shows. It renders skip links to the body and the navigation, the banner and main landmarks, keeps clear of the safe areas, moves focus to the form that shows when the form that had it hides, and, where the body scrolls, lets the keyboard scroll it as it would the document. `--env-scaffold-sticky-top` keeps sticky content in the body under the top bar, and where the document scrolls, its scroll padding is the bars' heights, which the Scaffold measures, so focus stays clear of them. `data-env-layout="scaffold"`, and the `env-scaffold` container. `resolveNavigationForms` returns the form of each window class.
