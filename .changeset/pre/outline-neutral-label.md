An outline Button and IconButton take the text color, `--env-color-fg-default`, for their label and icon, rather than the brand blue, so an outline Cancel beside a primary Rename reads as the other choice rather than a second blue action. A text button keeps the link blue, and a primary button the brand blue fill. Among an Alert's actions an outline button keeps the alert's text color.
