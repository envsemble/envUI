In dark, `--env-font-weight-medium` is 460 rather than 500: light text on a dark ground reads heavier, so headings and leading labels, such as a NavigationRail's, keep the weight they have in light. See [Typography](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/foundations/typography.md).
