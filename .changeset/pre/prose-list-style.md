Lists in formatted text, which Markdown, RichText and RichTextEditor render, take envUI's own markers instead of the browser's discs: a small circle in the selected fill on the middle of each item's first line, holding a dot for a bullet and the number for an ordered item, with a full step between items. A nested list steps down quietly, with closer items and a smaller marker without the fill, a muted dot or a letter. Task lists keep their checkboxes.
