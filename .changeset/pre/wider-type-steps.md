The title and headline type styles are a clear step apart from body text: a title is 18 px and a headline 22 px in the comfortable scale (16 and 20 px compact), with tighter lines and letters drawn a touch closer, so a dialog's, a card's or a page's heading reads as one at a glance. `--env-type-title-letter-spacing` and `--env-type-headline-letter-spacing` carry the tracking, which envUI's own headings set.
