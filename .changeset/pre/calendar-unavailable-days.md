The days of a Calendar or a DatePicker outside `min` and `max`, and the months of a MonthPicker outside them, take the disabled text color, `--env-color-fg-disabled`, as well as fading, rather than fading the text color alone, which left them only a little lighter than the days that can be chosen. Today, or this month, keeps its accent color, faded.
