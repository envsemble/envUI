The Button and Card documentation pages are on a new figure template: plain sections in a fixed order (Overview, Variants, When to use, Anatomy, Adapting to width, Accessibility), visual first, with short text that says each thing once. Do and don't figures are fences such as ` ```tsx do=OnePrimaryAction caption="…" `, and an anatomy figure's legend names each part by its class name. Every figure carries its example's whole source in the packaged pages, as examples do. The other component pages follow as they are converted.
