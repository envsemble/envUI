In a vertical RadioGroup, the chosen row shows its choice with the selection's fill and the filled radio, and its label keeps the text color rather than taking the selection's. On a surface of the rows' own color, a dialog's, a popover's or a muted or raised card's or Surface's, the rows take a faint tint of the neutral fill, so they keep their shape.
