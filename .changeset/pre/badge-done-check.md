A `done` Badge leads with a check mark unless it is given another `icon`, or `icon={null}` for none, and a soft one takes half its tint, fainter than a neutral badge, so completed work reads apart from neutral and primary. On a selected Table row or List item, a soft badge lays its tint over the raised surface, so it no longer sinks into the selection's blue.
