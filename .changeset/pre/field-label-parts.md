Field labels have part classes: `env-text-field-label` on TextField's and Textarea's label, `env-select-label` on Select's, `env-combobox-label` on Combobox's and `env-date-picker-label` on DatePicker's, MonthPicker's and TimePicker's, so a style or a test can address a label without an element selector.
