`size="sm"` on a TextField, SearchField, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, TimePicker or MonthPicker is for a field with its label hidden, as in a toolbar or a filter row. A small field that shows its label still takes the medium height, and now says so in the console while developing, so the size no longer seems to do nothing. See [TextField's sizes](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/text-field.md#sizes).
