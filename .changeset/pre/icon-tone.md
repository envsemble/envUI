Icons take `tone`: `primary`, `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `danger` or `done` colors the icon with the tone's text color, `--env-color-fg-{tone}`, such as a run step that is done or failed; `neutral`, the default, keeps the color of the text around it. `IconTone` is its type. See https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/foundations/icons.md.
