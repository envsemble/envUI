In the dark theme an avatar takes a deep fill of its hue with light initials and figure, rather than the light theme's pastel, so avatars no longer outshine everything else on a dark screen; at high contrast too, at 7:1. In a table's cell and a small AvatarGroup the fill still takes half its chroma. See [Avatar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/avatar.md).
