Buttons read as available or not at a glance. A secondary button's label is in the text color, so it no longer looks disabled on a grey or tinted surface, and a secondary button among an Alert's actions takes the tone's deeper tint. An unavailable primary button, in any tone, takes the neutral fill and label rather than a paler brand color, and an unavailable outline or text button the neutral label.
