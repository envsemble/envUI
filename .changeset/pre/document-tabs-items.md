DocumentTabs' items are `DocumentTabsItem`s, keyed by `value`, as `value` and `onValueChange` name the active tab, where `DocumentTab` had `id`; their native tooltip is `tooltip`, where it was `title`. DocumentTabs passes its native props, such as `id` and `data-*`, on to the strip. Breadcrumbs' item type is `BreadcrumbsItem`.
