A Calendar's days, on their own and in a DatePicker's popup, are a little less than a row height wide, 24 px at the compact scale and 32 px at the comfortable one, rather than a row height, so the columns stand close around the small numbers; the days of a week still touch, with a hair between the weeks, and touch screens keep touch-sized days. The time wheels of TimePicker and DatePicker keep a day's height, so a chosen day and time still look alike.
