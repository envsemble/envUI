The pattern pages keep their decision and their compositions, and link to each component for its own behaviour. The window frame is App frame's "An installed app's window", and the account menu and the organization switcher are one pattern, Account and organizations, at `docs/patterns/account-and-organizations.md`; their old addresses open the pages they joined. Each pattern's decision is a Which to use table. Form layout and validation adds a long form in steps, with Progress split into steps, and which control to choose for a choice or a date; Empty states adds a whole page, such as one not found. The content guidelines are the Writing guide, `docs/writing.md`: their labels and error messages live on Form layout and validation, their empty states on Empty states, their feedback on Notifications and feedback and their confirmations on Destructive actions.
