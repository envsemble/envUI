An outline Button, IconButton and Select trigger is just an edge: it has no fill at rest, so it reads the same on the page, a pane, a card or a popup, where it took the raised surface's fill before. Hover, press and an open Select lay the neutral state layer over the surface beneath it, as a ghost button does; the edge, the label's color and the danger tone are unchanged. A container or content color set on one button still applies as before.
