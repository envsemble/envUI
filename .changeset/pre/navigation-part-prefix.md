NavigationBar, NavigationRail and NavigationDrawer name their parts and color properties after themselves. Rename them in your style sheets and tests:

- NavigationBar: `env-bar`, `env-bar-list`, `env-bar-item`, `env-bar-indicator`, `env-bar-label`, `env-bar-count` and `env-bar-more` are `env-navigation-bar`, `env-navigation-bar-list`, `-item`, `-indicator`, `-label`, `-count` and `-more`; `--env-bar-item-container`, `--env-bar-item-content`, `--env-bar-item-icon`, `--env-bar-item-selected` and `--env-bar-item-selected-content` are `--env-navigation-bar-item-container`, `-content`, `-icon`, `-selected` and `-selected-content`.
- NavigationRail: `env-rail`, `env-rail-header`, `env-rail-list`, `env-rail-item`, `env-rail-indicator`, `env-rail-label`, `env-rail-count` and `env-rail-footer` are `env-navigation-rail`, `env-navigation-rail-header`, `-list`, `-item`, `-indicator`, `-label`, `-count` and `-footer`; `--env-rail-item-container`, `--env-rail-item-content`, `--env-rail-item-icon`, `--env-rail-item-selected` and `--env-rail-item-selected-content` are `--env-navigation-rail-item-container`, `-content`, `-icon`, `-selected` and `-selected-content`.
- NavigationDrawer: `env-drawer`, `env-drawer-header`, `env-drawer-list`, `env-drawer-section`, `env-drawer-group-label`, `env-drawer-item`, `env-drawer-label`, `env-drawer-count`, `env-drawer-branch`, `env-drawer-branch-row`, `env-drawer-toggle`, `env-drawer-chevron`, `env-drawer-children`, `env-drawer-footer` and `env-drawer-resize-handle` are `env-navigation-drawer`, `env-navigation-drawer-header`, `-list`, `-section`, `-group-label`, `-item`, `-label`, `-count`, `-branch`, `-branch-row`, `-toggle`, `-chevron`, `-children`, `-footer` and `-resize-handle`; `--env-drawer-item-container`, `--env-drawer-item-content`, `--env-drawer-item-icon`, `--env-drawer-item-selected` and `--env-drawer-item-selected-content` are `--env-navigation-drawer-item-container`, `-content`, `-icon`, `-selected` and `-selected-content`.

Their state attributes, `data-background` (`page`), `data-current`, `data-resizable`, `data-env-skeleton` and Base UI's `data-starting-style` and `data-ending-style`, are unchanged on the renamed parts.
