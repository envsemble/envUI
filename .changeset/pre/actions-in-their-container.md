Actions take the colors of the notice they sit in, so no second accent competes with its tone. A toast's action is an outline button in the toast's text color, with its edge in that color and the dismiss button's hover and press fills, rather than a blue primary button on the green, red or blue fill. In an Alert, outline, ghost and text buttons among the actions take the alert's text color, an outline's edge the tone's color, and the tone's layers on hover and press, rather than the link blue; a secondary button keeps its fill.
