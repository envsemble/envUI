Motion refinements: colors under the pointer change in `--env-motion-duration-fast` with `--env-motion-ease-enter` on Tabs' labels and TextField's edge and label; a Slider's fill and handle reach a value a press on the track chooses in `--env-motion-duration-fast`, and a pressed handle grows a little; an Accordion panel, a NavigationDrawer section and a Tree row's children fade over their whole closing, so no empty band is left; Select's and Combobox's chevron turns back as fast as the list closes; and a List row's link or button lays no pressed fill of its own inside its pressed row.
