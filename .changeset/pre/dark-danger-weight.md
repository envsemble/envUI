In dark, a danger primary Button or IconButton, and a solid danger Badge, is a saturated mid red with a white label, `--env-color-bg-danger-emphasis` with `--env-color-fg-on-strong`, rather than a pale coral with a dark label: beside the soft blue and lilac primaries a destructive action is now the deepest, weightiest fill, apart by lightness as well as hue. It lightens a step on hover and, pressed, deepens a small step below rest, as the other dark primaries do; the high-contrast dark theme takes a deep red with a white label too. Light is unchanged.
