In dark, primary, accent and danger primary buttons, and the checked checkboxes, radios and switches that take the primary fill, lighten on hover rather than deepen, so their dark label or mark gains contrast; light still darkens. A danger primary, and a solid danger badge, take a redder fill in dark than the accent’s (`--env-color-bg-danger-emphasis`), so Delete reads as danger beside the accent's lilac rather than as a second pastel pink.
