AvatarGroup keeps initials readable at rest: a group whose avatars show initials piles looser, so two letters stay whole under the next avatar, and a group of initials or a small group, as in a table's row, rests upright and in line rather than turned. The count of the rest is a flat circle the size of the avatars, which a larger count widens into a pill, rather than a shadowed rounded square.
