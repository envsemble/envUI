Composer: `top` places content inside the composer above the field, such as attachment chips, a quoted reply or context pills, and `end` places content in the action row just before the send button, such as a voice button, a model picker or a token count. `sendKey="mod-enter"` makes Ctrl+Enter or ⌘+Enter send and Enter start a new line, for prompts that mostly take several lines; Enter still sends by default. See [Composer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/composer.md).
