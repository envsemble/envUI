Message keys take the names of the props that override them: PanelDock's `messages.panelDock.show`, `hidden` and `closePanel` are `open`, `minimized` and `close`, after `openLabel`, `minimizedLabel` and `closeLabel`, and its header's Close control is `closeWindow`; DocumentTabs' and Tree's `actions` are `contextMenu`, after `contextMenuLabel`; TopBar's `loadingPage` is `loading`, after `loadingLabel`.
