Message: `author` takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as a link to a profile with a Bot or Owner Badge, and `statusLabel` takes content such as a sentence with a Try again link. A new `start` places content in a column beside the message, such as the author's Avatar or an assistant's mark. MessageList's `busyAuthor` and `busyLabel` take any content too, such as the agent's Avatar and name or the current step, while the log keeps reporting aria-busy. See [Message](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/message.md).
