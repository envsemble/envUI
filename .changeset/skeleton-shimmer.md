Skeletons shimmer instead of pulsing: a soft highlight sweeps across every bar and shape, laid out on the window so it crosses every skeleton on the page as one, in step, and keeps still with a reduced-motion preference. The highlight is a new token, `--env-color-skeleton-shine`. Bars for missing text have small rounded corners on every line, shapes keep their component's corners, and media without corners of its own takes small ones. A Card over a placeholder item keeps the size of its loaded text: its title and meta share one row and its description is one line.
