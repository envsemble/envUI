Card: a content card takes `interactive`, and a link or button inside it marked `data-row-target`, such as its title, stretches over the card and takes its click, as in a List row, while its `meta`, `actions` and the new `end` keep their own. A grid of project cards that open on click can keep a title row, a status Badge and an overflow Menu. `end` puts content at the end of the title row, after the meta, such as an Edit Button or a Menu. `description` is no longer a paragraph, so a list or a row of Badges is valid there; its look is unchanged. See [Card](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/card.md).
