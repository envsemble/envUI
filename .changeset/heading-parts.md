"heading" names one part: the column of a title and its description, as in Dialog, PageHeader and PanelDock. The other `-heading` parts are renamed for what they are:

- a title: Card's `env-card-heading` is `env-card-title`, Calendar's `env-calendar-heading` `env-calendar-title`, MonthPicker's `env-month-picker-heading` `env-month-picker-title` and DatePicker's `env-date-picker-time-heading` `env-date-picker-time-title`;
- the row of a label and a value, or of a label and a button: Meter's `env-meter-heading`, Progress's `env-progress-heading`, Slider's `env-slider-heading` and CodeBlock's `env-code-block-heading` are `env-meter-header`, `env-progress-header`, `env-slider-header` and `env-code-block-header`;
- a group's label, as in Menu and Combobox: Drawer's `env-drawer-heading` is `env-drawer-group-label` and CommandPalette's `env-command-palette-heading` is `env-command-palette-group-label`.
