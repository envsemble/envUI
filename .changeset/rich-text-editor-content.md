RichTextEditor: a reference source takes `renderSuggestion`, which gives a reference's row in the suggestion list `start`, `description` and `end` content, such as each person's Avatar and e-mail address; a slash command takes an `icon` and a `description`. `toolbarEnd` adds the application's own Toolbar.Button or Toolbar.Item controls at the toolbar's end, in its arrow-key path, and `footer` places content inside the editor's box under the document, such as a comment box's Send button. See [RichText](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/rich-text.md).
