Kbd's edge is the text color at 5 %, translucent like its fill, so a key looks the same on any surface: the page, a pane or a card, in light and dark. Its fixed gray edge disappeared on the light theme's gray page and stood out on lighter surfaces. At high contrast the edge takes the border color.

Keys in a button's end slot, such as a search button's shortcut, are drawn the same way from the button's label color: a faint fill of it with an edge just stronger, in place of an outlined key on no fill.
