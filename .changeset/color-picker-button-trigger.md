ColorPickerButton: `trigger` opens the picker from the application's own element in place of the swatch button, such as a toolbar's text color button with a bar of the color, given as an element or a function of the color's value; it takes the native props, the opening behavior and, unless it has its own, the name after the label and the color, and `Toolbar.Item` puts it in a toolbar's arrow-key path. It also takes the picker's new `swatchesFirst` and `fields`. See [ColorPicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/color-picker.md).
