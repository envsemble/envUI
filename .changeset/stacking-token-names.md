The stacking levels say what they hold: `--env-z-popover`, for menus, popovers, hover cards and the lists of Select and Combobox, is `--env-z-popup`, and `--env-z-peek`, for a supporting pane over its main pane, is `--env-z-supporting-pane`. `v1-classes` renames them.
