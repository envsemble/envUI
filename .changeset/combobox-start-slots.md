Combobox names its start slots as other components do: the icon at the start of the field or an option, `env-combobox-leading`, is `env-combobox-start`, and the chosen option's icon in the field, `env-combobox-value-leading`, is `env-combobox-value-icon`. `v1-classes` renames both.
