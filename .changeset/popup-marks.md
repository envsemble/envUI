Popup lists mark each thing once: the option or item the pointer or the keyboard is on takes the neutral fill, with no focus ring of its own, and the chosen one in a Select, a Combobox or a menu's radio group shows its check mark without the selection's fill. In dark, frosted popups take a faint hairline edge, so they keep their edge over a card of their own shade. The time wheels of DatePicker and TimePicker show the chosen time in the selection's tint rather than three solid pills.
