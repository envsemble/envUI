Panes.Trigger, a Button in the main pane that opens the supporting pane while it is a sheet, below `splitAt`. It says whether the sheet is open with `aria-expanded`, names the pane in `aria-controls`, and hides while the panes sit side by side, where the supporting pane shows anyway, so a Details button no longer lingers beside the details. Its part is `env-panes-trigger`.
