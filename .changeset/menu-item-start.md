Menu: `Menu.Item`, `Menu.CheckboxItem`, `Menu.RadioItem` and `Menu.Submenu` take `start`, content before the label in place of an icon, such as an Avatar in an account switcher, at its own size; the labels of every row of the menu still line up, icons included. See [Menu](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/menu.md).
