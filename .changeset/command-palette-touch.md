Under a coarse pointer, CommandPalette hides the keys, its items' shortcuts and the key hints of its footer, which then shows only the application's own content, and shows a close button after the search field, `env-command-palette-close`, for touch screens that have no Esc, named by `messages.dialog.close`. The search and the button share a row, `env-command-palette-header`.
