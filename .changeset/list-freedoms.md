List: a titled List.Root takes `actions`, content at the end of the title's line and outside the heading, such as an Add button, a Manage link or a count. In an interactive List.Item, links, buttons and fields in the label, the description or the children, such as an author's link, now stay clickable above the stretched `data-row-target`, as in Table. `handlePlacement="end"` puts a reorderable list's drag handles at the end of each row. See [List](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/list.md).
