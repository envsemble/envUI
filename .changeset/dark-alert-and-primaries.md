Every primary button takes its tone's emphasis fill, as the default primary does: an accent primary keeps its saturated fill with a white label in light and takes a soft fill with a dark label in dark, so it is never louder than Continue beside it. The new tokens are `--env-color-bg-danger-emphasis-hover` and `-pressed`, and `--env-color-bg-accent-emphasis` with its `-hover` and `-pressed`. A count or diff stat in a danger primary button is an outlined pill in its label color, as in the other primaries.

In dark, a warning alert rests on its softer tint, where its amber title and icon sank into the stronger one, and a secondary button among its actions is a lift of the text color rather than a tan tint.
