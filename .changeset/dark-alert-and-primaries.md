In dark, an alert rests on its tone's softer tint, with the tone left to its icon and title, and a secondary button among its actions is a neutral lift of the text color rather than a deeper tint of the tone, so a warning no longer reads as three ambers on brown. Danger and accent primary buttons in dark take their tone's light fill with dark text, as the default primary does, so Delete is never louder than Continue beside it; in light they are unchanged. A count or diff stat in a danger primary button is an outlined pill in its text color, as in the other primaries.
