A resizable Drawer keeps each label and group heading on one line, so its items keep their height as people narrow it: one that doesn't fit ends in an ellipsis. A label cut short shows in full in a tooltip beside the drawer, on hover and keyboard focus; a heading cut short, which takes no focus, gives its full text as its `title`, shown on hover. A Drawer that isn't resizable still wraps a long label.
