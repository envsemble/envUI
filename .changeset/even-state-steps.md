Interaction states step evenly: a control without a fill, such as a ghost button or an inactive tab, hovers a lighter layer, so its press stands apart, and a labelled ghost button hovers the same layer as an icon-only one; the neutral fill of secondary buttons hovers a clearer step from rest; and a primary action presses one grade past its hover rather than two.
