The light theme separates elements a little more clearly, with the same hues and lightness. Dividers and the edges of popups, tables and other decorative outlines (`--env-color-border-muted`) are a step darker, about 1.5:1 on white rather than 1.4:1. The translucent fill of secondary buttons, tab rails, chips, switch and meter tracks (`--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`) is a layer of 9% rather than 6%. Muted text (`--env-color-fg-muted`, with its hover and pressed states) is a step darker, 7.3:1 on white rather than 6.2:1. The dark theme and both high-contrast themes are unchanged, and no token is renamed.
