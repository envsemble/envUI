The light theme's backgrounds step apart more clearly, with the same white page and cool grays. Surfaces (`--env-color-bg-default`), and list rows with them, are a step grayer, and cards and other muted surfaces (`--env-color-bg-muted`) take the gray surfaces had, so each level differs from the next about twice as much as before; raised surfaces, popups and fields stay white. The translucent neutral fill of secondary buttons, tab rails and chips (`--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`) is an 8% layer rather than 6%, dividers and popup edges (`--env-color-border-muted`) are a step darker, and the selection is a step stronger. So that text keeps 4.5:1 on the grayer surfaces, link, accent, status and muted text are one grade darker. The dark theme and both high-contrast themes are unchanged, and no token is renamed.
