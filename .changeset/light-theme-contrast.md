The light theme's backgrounds mirror the dark theme's, so the same elements stand apart in both: what is lighter than its surroundings in dark is grayer in light. The page stays white; the default and raised surfaces (`--env-color-bg-default`, `--env-color-bg-raised`) and popups (`--env-color-bg-popup`) are a cool gray step from it, and cards, other muted surfaces (`--env-color-bg-muted`) and list rows (`--env-color-list-item-bg`, now the muted surface in both themes) a further step, so a card stands apart from the page about four times as clearly as before. Hover and pressed states, the translucent neutral fill of secondary buttons, tab rails and chips (`--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`, now 10%) and dividers (`--env-color-border-muted`) follow. So that text keeps 4.5:1 on the grayer surfaces, link text (`--env-color-fg-link`, `--env-color-fg-link-on-tint`), muted text (`--env-color-fg-muted`) and the pressed edge of an invalid field are one grade darker; the accent, primary, selection and focus blues are unchanged. The dark theme and both high-contrast themes are unchanged, and no token is renamed.
