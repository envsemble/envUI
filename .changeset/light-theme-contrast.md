The light theme's backgrounds step apart more clearly, with the same cool hue. The page (`--env-color-bg-page`) is a pale cool gray rather than white, and the surfaces grow lighter as they rise: the default surface (`--env-color-bg-default`) is near-white, and cards and other muted surfaces (`--env-color-bg-muted`) are white, as raised surfaces, popups and fields are. Cards stand apart from the page about three times as clearly as before, and dialogs, tables and popups now differ from the page by their color as well as their edge. The surfaces' hover and pressed states, dividers (`--env-color-border-muted`) and the selection (`--env-color-bg-selected`) follow the new steps. To keep 4.5:1 on the tinted page, link, accent, status and muted text are one grade darker. The dark theme and both high-contrast themes are unchanged, and no token is renamed.
