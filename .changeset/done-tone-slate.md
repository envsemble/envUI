The `done` tone, a Badge's color for completed work, is a cyan of its own rather than a violet, so a done badge no longer looks like an accent one at badge size: `--env-color-fg-done`, `--env-color-bg-done-emphasis`, `--env-color-bg-done-muted` and `--env-color-border-done` change in every theme, at the same contrast.
