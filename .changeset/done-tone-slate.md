The `done` tone, a Badge's color for completed work, is a quiet slate blue-gray of its own rather than a violet or a cyan: completed things recede, and a done badge stays apart from an accent one, a success one and a primary one at table size. `--env-color-fg-done`, `--env-color-bg-done-emphasis`, `--env-color-bg-done-muted` and `--env-color-border-done` change in every theme, at the same contrast.
