New: `LineArt`, a geometric drawing in hairlines for a banner's end, an empty state or the background of a header, such as a golden rectangle cut into squares or a chain of circles between three arcs. `drawing` names one of sixteen, listed in order as `lineArtDrawings`, such as `golden-section` or `harmonic-division`. It fills its container's box, covering it: `align` is `end` (the default), `start` or `center`, where the drawing sits as the box crops it, mirrored in right-to-left text; it always rests on the box's foot, cropped from its top. Its lines are the muted edge color, `--env-color-border-muted`, a hairline at any size, and `GrayText` in forced colors. It is hidden from assistive technology, or with `label` an image named by it. The root is `env-line-art`, an SVG with `data-drawing` and `data-align`. With `motion`, its lines draw themselves one beat after another in the order of the drawing's construction: `appear` draws them in, `disappear` draws them out, `loop` does both by turns, each turn beginning where the last one ends, and `play` runs each line out and in again while the drawing stays whole, ending on the still. `onMotionEnd` is called when `appear`, `disappear` or `play` finishes. A motion takes `--env-line-art-duration`, ten of `--env-motion-duration-slow` by default; it moves only the lines' dashes, so they stay hairlines and nothing around them moves. Where reduced motion is preferred, a motion shows where it ends at once, and a loop stays whole.
