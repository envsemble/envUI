A focused outline Button or IconButton shows one outline: its ring lies on its edge, which takes the focus color, rather than a blue ring a hair outside a gray or red edge. In dark, a focused primary button's ring takes the text color, `--env-color-fg-default`, so it stands apart from the soft blue fill of its own hue. An Alert's dismiss button draws its focus ring inside its circle, the size of its hover fill, rather than around it.
