Button: `start` places content before the label at its own size rather than the icon's, such as a small Avatar of the person a button assigns, hidden from assistive technology as the label names the button. `render` renders the button as another element, such as a label for a file input or a router's own link in one place: the button's class, look, state attributes, content and handlers merge into its props, and a disabled one reports aria-disabled and blocks presses. See [Button](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/button.md).
