TopBar, the bar at the top of an application or a screen, built from Row: start content, a title that truncates first and actions, at the top bar height (`--env-top-bar-height`). `sticky` keeps it at the top as the page scrolls, `loading` shows page progress along its bottom edge, and `titleBar` makes it the window's own title bar in an installed web app.
