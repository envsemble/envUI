In Menu, ContextMenu, Menubar menus and the rows of CommandPalette, Select and Combobox, a shortcut, or a Kbd at a row's end, is muted text in one unit, such as Ctrl+D, rather than a filled chip for each key, so a list of shortcuts stays quiet down its rows; on a highlighted row it takes the text color. Kbd elsewhere, such as in a button's end, a tooltip or the command palette's key hints, keeps its key caps.
