The compact scale's control heights are 28, 36 and 44 px for `sm`, `md` and `lg` (`--env-control-height-sm`, `-md` and `-lg`), up from 26, 32 and 38, so a labelled field and a button of one size share one height and `size` sets a step you can see. Every control takes them: buttons, fields, Select, Combobox, the pickers, SegmentedControl, Pagination, the rail of Tabs and the controls of toolbars. Menus and the lists of Select and Combobox keep their rows at 32 px, as dense as before. The comfortable scale is unchanged. See [Spacing & density](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/foundations/spacing.md#sizes-in-each-scale).
