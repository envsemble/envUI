Avatars stay quiet in dense places: in a table's cell and in a small AvatarGroup the fills take half their chroma at the same lightness, so the initials keep their contrast, and a small group, or one in a table, overlaps less, initials only by the ring that parts them, so their letters no longer touch. In an AvatarGroup, an avatar whose color looks like the one before it, such as two pinks, takes the next color that doesn't. See [Avatar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/avatar.md).
