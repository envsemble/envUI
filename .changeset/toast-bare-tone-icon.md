A Toast's tone icon stands on its own, in its tone's color, without the disc behind it, at a control's icon size, 20 px, and level with the first line of the message rather than with the middle of the toast, so a message of two or three lines starts beside its icon. The icon and the message sit together in a new part, `env-toast-content`.
