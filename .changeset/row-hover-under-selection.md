Tree and table rows under the pointer take a state layer of their own, `--env-color-bg-row-hover` and `--env-color-bg-row-pressed`: in light a step quieter than a control's, so a hovered row no longer weighs as much as the selected one on the gray page, and the selected row always reads strongest. Dark and high contrast keep their row states.
