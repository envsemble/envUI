AvatarGroup: each person takes a `hue`, a `badge` such as a presence dot, and a `tooltip` for a pressable avatar, such as the name with a role or an e-mail address. `renderOverflowTooltip` gives the overflow chip's tooltip the application's own content, such as the hidden people with their avatars. Accessible names stay the people's names, and the pile lays out and spreads as before. See [Avatar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/avatar.md).
