Menu.Item takes `tone="danger"` for a destructive action, such as Delete: its label and icon take the danger color, while its row highlights as any other's, and the item carries `data-tone`. A count at an item's end, a Badge with `count`, shows as its figures alone, in its tone's color without the pill, so it can't be taken for a shortcut's keys. An unavailable item dims its label, icon and end, while its description, which says why, keeps the muted text color and stays readable.
