Bar, Rail and Drawer are NavigationBar, NavigationRail and NavigationDrawer, with `NavigationBarProps`, `NavigationRailProps`, `NavigationDrawerProps` and `NavigationDrawerDestination`, after Material's navigation bar, rail and drawer: elsewhere a drawer is a panel over the page, and Bar named nothing in particular. Their part classes stay `env-bar-*`, `env-rail-*` and `env-drawer-*`, and their style sheets are `NavigationBar.css`, `NavigationRail.css` and `NavigationDrawer.css`. The documentation page is `navigation-drawer`; `drawer` opens it.
