Scaffold takes `end`, a region at the end edge beside the body, such as an inspector, and `bottomBar`, a bar along the bottom, such as a status bar. Both stay in view while the document scrolls, as the rail and the top bar do: the bottom bar sits above a NavigationBar, and focus scrolls clear of it. Without them the layout is unchanged. See [Scaffold](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/scaffold.md).
