Select: an option takes `start` content before its label, such as a status dot, a priority's icon, a flag or an Avatar, which the trigger also shows before the chosen label, and `end` content in the list, such as a count or a Badge. `items` also takes groups, `{ label, items }`, each under a heading that names it for assistive technology. The label stays the text people type ahead on and the trigger shows. See [Select](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/select.md).
