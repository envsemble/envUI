Alert, Badge and Toast take the spacing and type of the approved Tonal design. An Alert's inset is 14 px above and below and 16 px at the sides, its title semibold in the body size 2 px above its message, which takes the small body size. A Badge has 3 px above and below its label and 10 px at the sides, edge included. A Toast has 16 px before its icon or message and 10 px between its parts.
