Accordion: an item takes `end`, content beside its heading and outside its button, such as a Switch, a Badge with a Menu, or a Delete IconButton. `indicator` replaces the plus and minus with the application's own, such as a chevron, or `null` for none, and `indicatorPosition="end"` moves it after the title. See [Accordion](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/accordion.md).
