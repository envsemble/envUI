NavigationRail: `labels="tooltip"` makes an icon-only rail, such as a code editor's activity bar: each label shows only in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, and still names its destination. A destination's `label` also takes content, such as a name with a small Beta mark, with its text in `textValue`; `itemProps` sets more props on its link or button, such as `target`, `rel` or data attributes; `disabled` dims it and takes it out of reach; and a `count` of true shows a dot with no number, named as Activity, new, from the new `messages.navigation.itemNew`. These destination fields work in NavigationBar and NavigationDrawer too. The type of a destination's `label` widens from `string` to `ReactNode`, and its `count` from `number` to `number | true`: destinations an app passes in keep working, and code that reads `label` back as a string, or `count` as a number, needs a check that it is one. See [NavigationDrawer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/navigation-drawer.md).
