Breadcrumbs: a crumb takes `start`, a decorative graphic before its label, such as a small Avatar of a project. It sits inside the link or button, centred on the label's line, and is never cut short; it is hidden from assistive technology, so the label still names the crumb and fills its tooltip. See [Breadcrumbs](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/breadcrumbs.md).
