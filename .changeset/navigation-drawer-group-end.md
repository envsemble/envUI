NavigationDrawer: `groupEnd` puts content after a group's heading, outside it, such as an IconButton that adds a project to Projects or a Menu of the group's actions. A destination's new `textValue`, `itemProps`, `disabled` and `count` of true work in the drawer too, and a section's button takes its name from `textValue` where the label is content. See [NavigationDrawer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/navigation-drawer.md).
