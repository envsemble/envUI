Text with more on hover, a Tooltip's or HoverCard's text trigger and RelativeTime, takes a clearer dotted underline: larger round dots, spaced further apart, drawn by one rule, `env-hover-detail`, and the pointer stays the arrow over it rather than the text cursor. RelativeTime's time with a tooltip now takes the underline, as its documentation said; before, its own part class kept it off.
