CommandPalette: an item takes `start`, content before its label in place of an icon, such as a person's Avatar among the results, at its own size, and `groupLabel`, the heading's content where it differs from `group`, such as an icon or a count beside the group's name, while `group` stays the key that starts a section. `searchStart` and `searchEnd` place content in the search row, before the field and after it, such as a scope chip or a mode switch. See [CommandPalette](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/command-palette.md).
