Message namespaces are named after the component or concept they serve: CopyButton's `copy` messages are `copyButton`, and `themeToggle`, which ThemeToggle and ThemeMenuRadioGroup share, is `themePreference`. `common.clear` is `combobox.clear`, as only Combobox reads it, and the unused `common.more` is gone; `common` holds `loading`.
