A Table's stacked rows are compact blocks: the first cell titles the row, with the first labelled value, such as a status, beside it without its label, and an unlabelled cell, such as the row's menu of actions, on the same line; the other values follow two to a line, each under its label, in place of a line for each value. Put a value that reads on its own, such as a status, second. [Table](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/table.md#stacked-rows)
