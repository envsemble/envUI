RichTextEditor takes `minRows`, the lines of text its document is tall enough for while it holds fewer, such as `minRows={2}` for a short note in a card; without it the document is at least 10 rem tall, as before. See [RichText](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/rich-text.md).
