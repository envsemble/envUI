TableOfContents marks the reader's place with one shape down the side of the list instead of a dash before each section: straight along the list's start and swelling toward the labels, widest beside the current section and thin a few sections away, in a slider's track color, with a slider's fill over it beside the sections passed, down to the swell's apex. It rests on each section and glides on to the next as the page scrolls, at once where reduced motion is preferred, swells a little under the pointer, and takes the system's gray text color in forced colors, with its text color beside the sections passed. It is `env-table-of-contents-wave`, an SVG hidden from assistive technology, `--env-table-of-contents-stroke` wide at its fullest. Inside a transformed or zoomed container, such as a scaled preview, the current section is measured in the scrolling area's own pixels, where it was once marked early or late.
