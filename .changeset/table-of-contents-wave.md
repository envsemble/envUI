TableOfContents marks the reader's place with one shape down the side of the list, like a brush stroke, instead of a dash before each section: widest and in the text color beside the current section, running thin and quieter a few sections away, darker beside the sections passed than beside those to come. It rests on each section and glides on to the next as the page scrolls, at once where reduced motion is preferred, swells a little under the pointer, and takes the system's text color in forced colors. It is `env-table-of-contents-wave`, an SVG hidden from assistive technology, `--env-table-of-contents-stroke` wide at its fullest. Inside a transformed or zoomed container, such as a scaled preview, the current section is measured in the scrolling area's own pixels, where it was once marked early or late.
