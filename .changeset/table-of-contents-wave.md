TableOfContents marks the reader's place with a pill on a thin track down the side of the list instead of a dash before each section: the track in `--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`, and the pill in `--env-color-bg-primary-emphasis`, as tall as the current section's label and centred beside it, while the label takes the link color, `--env-color-fg-link`. The labels of the passed sections stay in the text color and those to come are quieter. The pill glides to the next section on a spring as the page scrolls, drawn out a little as it travels, and comes to rest without overshooting; at once where reduced motion is preferred. In forced colors the pill takes the system's selection color and the track its gray. It is `env-table-of-contents-wave`, an SVG hidden from assistive technology, 0.375 rem wide. Inside a transformed or zoomed container, such as a scaled preview, the current section is measured in the scrolling area's own pixels, where it was once marked early or late.
