TableOfContents marks the reader's place with one shape down the side of the list instead of a dash before each section, in one quiet color, `--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`: straight along the list's start and bulging broadly toward the labels beside the current section, thin a few sections away, and shorter in a short list; the labels alone tell the sections passed from those to come. It rests on each section and moves on to the next as the page scrolls, on a spring: it stretches and leans back as it travels and settles with a short wobble as it lands, then stays still; at once, without the wobble, where reduced motion is preferred. It swells a little under the pointer, where the section's label takes the accent's text color, and takes the system's text color in forced colors. It is `env-table-of-contents-wave`, an SVG hidden from assistive technology, `--env-table-of-contents-stroke` wide at its fullest, now `--env-space-5` by default, so the labels sit a little further in. Inside a transformed or zoomed container, such as a scaled preview, the current section is measured in the scrolling area's own pixels, where it was once marked early or late.
