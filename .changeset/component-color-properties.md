Any single element can take colors of its own, Material's way.

- **Component color properties.** Each component has public color properties on its root, named `--env-<component>-<role>`, after Material's component tokens: container, content, outline and icon where they apply, selected and selected-content for what is chosen, current or on, and indicator for a field's focused edge and a slider's fill, such as `--env-button-container`, `--env-list-item-selected` or `--env-text-field-indicator`. Set them on one instance through `style` or a selector that matches it: they are registered so they don't inherit, so one set on a wrapper colors nothing inside it. Button, IconButton and a toolbar's toggles, Card, Surface, Badge, Alert, Avatar, Tooltip, Toast, List rows, Menu items, the items of Bar, Rail and Drawer, Tabs, SegmentedControl, TextField, Textarea, Select, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Slider and Combobox's chips have them. They are public and versioned: `docs/generated/parts.json` lists them with the parts, with what each colors, the manifest and the MCP server's `get_component` return them, and each component's page names them under Anatomy.
- **States are state layers.** Hover, keyboard focus and press lay the content color over the container at the new tokens `--env-state-hover-opacity`, `--env-state-focus-opacity` and `--env-state-pressed-opacity`, so a component given its own container and content keeps working states. Keyboard focus now shows a layer under the focus ring. The default colors look as before, within a step; the solid fills, such as a primary button's, and the navigation's items keep their designed hover and pressed tokens until their container is set.
- **A nested Theme still recolors an area:** override the semantic `--env-color-*` tokens on it, with their hover and pressed values. [Customize colors](docs/customizing-colors.md) describes both ways.
- **Renamed component tokens.** `--env-list-item-fill` is `--env-list-item-container`; `--env-alert-tint`, `--env-alert-edge` and `--env-alert-tone` are `--env-alert-container`, `--env-alert-outline` and `--env-alert-icon`; `--env-toast-fill` and `--env-toast-edge` are `--env-toast-container` and `--env-toast-outline`. The hover and pressed component tokens that state layers replace, `--env-list-item-fill-hover` and `-pressed`, `--env-toast-fill-hover` and `-pressed`, `--env-surface-hover` and `-pressed` and `--env-alert-hover` and `-pressed`, are gone. `envui-codemods v1-classes` renames the first and reports the others, also where a renamed class gives one of the old names, and the migration guide lists them.
