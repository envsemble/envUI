Fields are rounded rectangles rather than pills: TextField, SearchField, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, TimePicker, MonthPicker and Field.Control take a text area's large corner, `--env-radius-lg`, no more than a third of the field's height, so a small field still reads as a rectangle. Buttons keep their round ends. The notched label is unchanged: the outline runs level into the notch past the corner. A ghost field takes the same corners, so a row of outline and ghost fields shares one corner; the command palette's search stays a pill. An editable ghost field shows a faint fill at rest, half the neutral fill, so a labelled one no longer reads as a label and its value; a read-only or disabled one keeps a fainter fill, quieter than one to fill in, and doesn't answer the pointer. With no edge to cut a notch in, a ghost field's label floats inside its fill, above the value, rather than across its top edge: a labelled single-line ghost field keeps the label, at the caption size, within its height, so it is as tall as an outline field of its size, and a ghost Textarea starts its text below the label. A focused ghost field keeps its placeholder, as an outline field does. Outline fields keep their notched label.
