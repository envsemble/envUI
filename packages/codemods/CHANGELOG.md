# @envsemble/envui-codemods

## 1.0.0-prerelease.0

### Major Changes

- First release of `@envsemble/envui-codemods`, which moves an application from envUI 0.x to the 1.0 names. `envui-codemods v1-names <paths>` rewrites imports, JSX, values, types and `typeof` from `@envsemble/envui`, merges compound parts into namespace imports (`MenuItem` becomes `Menu.Item`), turns `DirectionLink` into `Pager.Previous` or `Pager.Next`, turns `<Theme theme={useTheme()}>` into `<Theme {...useTheme()}>` and other `useTheme()` calls into `useTheme().theme`, the color scheme they returned in 0.x, unless the file already reads them as `{ theme, contrast }`, and reports the exports that became internal with what to use instead, a `useTheme()` call that is returned or passed to a context, and each `require()` or `import()` of envUI, which it does not follow. The codemods are meant for 0.x code, run once. `envui-codemods v1-classes <paths>` renames `env-*` classes and custom properties in style sheets, `className` values and selector strings. Both take `--dry`, `--print` and `--ignore-pattern`, and read the rename map in `src/maps/v1.json`.

  Migration guide: [Upgrade from envUI 0.x to 1.0](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/migration.md#upgrade-from-envui-0x-to-10).

- `envui-codemods v1-props <paths>` moves the props 1.0 removed or changed, from the new `props` section of the rename map: `density`, `compact` and size-as-density become sizes, row densities or nothing. `<Select density="compact">` becomes `size="sm"`, `<Table.Root density="compact">` `density="sm"`, `<Tree density="standard">` `density="md"`, `<Tabs size="compact">` `size="sm"`, `<TextField compact>` `showLabel={false}`, `compact={false}` `variant="floating"`, and `<DropZone compact>` `size="sm"`. Removed props without a replacement, such as Surface's and the menus' `density` or NavList.Item's `size`, are dropped with a report that suggests `scale="compact"` on a Theme or `size="sm"`; a value it can't read, such as an expression, stays and is reported. It recognises elements under their 0.x and 1.0 names, so it runs before or after `v1-names`.

  Migration guide: [Upgrade from envUI 0.x to 1.0](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/migration.md#upgrade-from-envui-0x-to-10).

- envUI's CSS moves into cascade layers, and every token takes the `--env-` prefix.
  - **Cascade layers.** Every rule of `styles.css` is in a layer of `env`: `env.reset`, `env.tokens`, `env.base`, `env.components` and `env.utilities`, in that order. An application's CSS outside cascade layers overrides envUI's whatever its specificity, so selectors and `!important` that only out-weighed envUI's rules can go. An application puts its resets and element styles in a layer before `env` and its own layers after it, declared once, such as `@layer reset, env, app;`: an element rule outside layers would restyle envUI's components too. The font face stays outside layers.
  - **The `--env-` prefix.** Every token is a custom property with the prefix: `--space-4` is `--env-space-4` and `--color-fg-muted` is `--env-color-fg-muted`, in Tokens.css, the token reference, the manifest and the MCP server. The application hooks take it too: `--env-line-height-body`, `--env-avatar-group-ring` and the pill corner `--env-radius-full`.
  - **The 0.7 aliases are removed.** `--color-text-muted`, `--color-row`, `--color-checkbox` and the other 0.7 names that were aliases of the semantic roles are gone; use the role that replaces each. The list item's per-theme fill and the avatar's fixed blues are component tokens now: `--env-color-list-item-bg` with `-hover` and `-pressed`, and `--env-color-avatar-bg`, `-fg` and `-figure`. `--md-sys-shape-corner-*` is retired for one radius scale, `--env-radius-{none|xs|sm|md|lg|xl|full}`, with `--env-radius-surface` and the row radii as its roles; `--radius-standard` is `--env-radius-sm` and `--radius-large` `--env-radius-lg`.
  - **Consistent color names.** A tone's edges grow from `border-{tone}-muted`, an alert's faint edge, through `border-{tone}`, an outlined badge's translucent edge, to the solid `border-accent-emphasis` and `border-danger-emphasis`, the edges of a focused and an invalid field, which were `border-accent` and `border-danger`. `border-translucent` is `border-media` and `bg-page-muted` is `bg-inset`, named for their roles, and the tones' state layers gain a resting `bg-{tone}-subtle`.
  - **Forced colors.** Under `forced-colors: active`, such as the system's contrast themes, selected, chosen, current, highlighted, checked, focused, invalid and unavailable states take system colors where a fill or shadow carried them, so they stay visible, and swatches, the color picker and calendar heatmaps keep their colors.
  - **Motion** lives in each component's style sheet; it is unchanged, with fades always and movement only without a reduced-motion preference.

  To migrate, run `npx @envsemble/envui-codemods v1-classes src`: it rewrites the old custom properties in style sheets and scripts, declared or read, to their 1.0 names, the 0.7 aliases straight to the tokens that replace them, and reports `--color-white`, `--color-canvas-dark` and the removed names with what to use instead. The migration guide lists every name. The ESLint plugin and the Stylelint config suggest the prefixed tokens.

  Migration guide: [Cascade layers](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/migration.md#cascade-layers) and [Tokens](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/migration.md#tokens).

### Minor Changes

- A new `v1-app-shell` transform rewrites 0.x's AppShell into the frame's parts, after `v1-names` names it `AppShell.Root`: `navigation` and `navigationLabel` become a `Sidebar.Root` with a `NavList.Root` in `Sidebar.Content`, `brand` a `SidebarTrigger` with its `mark`, placed with `context` in `Sidebar.Header` or, for `topbar="full"` (`headerSpan="full"`), in `AppShell.Header`'s `start`, `title` and `actions` the header's children and `end`, `sidebarContent` a `Sidebar.Content fill`, and the page `AppShell.Main`. `storageKey` is `persist`, the collapsed props are negated into the open ones, and pixel widths become rem. AppShellSkeleton becomes the same parts with `NavList.Skeleton` in a LoadingState, and `const { closeNarrowSidebar } = useAppShell()` becomes `useSidebar()`'s `dismiss`. `v1-classes` renames the frame's classes and reports those 1.0 drops, with the root's state attributes, and the 0.x sidebar width properties.
- `envui-codemods v1-props` renames the properties of the objects in an array written in a prop, from the new `each` rules of the rename map's `props` section: an organization's `name` and `detail` on OrganizationSwitcher and AccountMenu.OrganizationSubmenu, and a Combobox option's `leading`, also in a group's `items`. It reports such a prop whose items it can't read, such as a variable.
- The rename map's new `classReports` section lists the classes 1.0 neither keeps nor renames, and the selectors 1.0's markup no longer matches. `v1-classes` leaves them and reports each one with what to use instead: OrganizationSwitcher's `env-organization-menu-radio` and `-radio-dot`, which a shorter entry no longer renames to classes that don't exist, and a selector that puts `.env-document-tabs-close` inside `.env-document-tabs-tab`. `env-menu-theme` becomes `env-popup-theme`.
- The rename map covers Card, Badge, Avatar, Stat, SegmentedControl and the logos: `v1-structure` turns ActionCard and FeatureCard into a Card and drops or converts Card's `as`, and a structure case can add an element prop, such as `render={<article />}`; `v1-names` renames ClickableCard, InfoChip and SegmentedControlOption and reports BrandLogo and EnvUILogo; `v1-props` moves Card's and Stat.Group's `tone`, the avatar sizes, `detail`, `options` and Badge's `subtle`; `v1-classes` renames the Card family's, InfoChip's, the segments', the avatar sizes' and Stat's classes. The ESLint plugin's `no-renamed-exports` knows the renamed and removed names.
- The codemods cover what a run over real applications left to hand work:
  - `v1-props` maps each literal of a conditional value, also in nested ternaries and beside `&&`, `||` and `??`: `tone={failed ? 'error' : 'info'}` becomes `tone={failed ? 'danger' : 'info'}`, and a value 1.0 drops as the default becomes `undefined`. It deletes Code's `appearance`, Markdown's `inlineCode` and CodeBlock's `theme`, which 0.7 removed, and reports one that is an expression.
  - `v1-render` turns a ternary of element names into a ternary of elements: `as={section ? 'h2' : 'h1'}` becomes `render={section ? <h2 /> : <h1 />}`.
  - `v1-classes` rewrites the uses of a removed custom property that one token replaces as it is, rather than reporting them: `var(--control-height-small)` and `var(--icon-button-size)` become `var(--env-control-height)`, `--control-height-compact` `--env-control-height-sm`, and `--color-surface-base` and the other unused 0.7 colors their roles. A declaration is rewritten only where the old name is the one that becomes the token, the only one or the token's own name: where 1.0 merges several names into one token, as `--control-height`, `--control-height-small` and `--icon-button-size`, or a 0.7 alias and its role, a declaration of any other of them stays and is reported, since rewriting it would set the token for every component that reads it, and no block gets two declarations of one property. It renames classes and tokens in CSS-in-JS templates tagged `css`, `keyframes`, `createGlobalStyle`, `injectGlobal` or `styled`, in test locators (`locator()`, `waitForSelector()`, `$()`), `toHaveClass()` and `expect(el.classList)` assertions, and, in test files, a string that is exactly one 0.x class passed to a call that names classes, such as `findByClass()`, or one class selector passed to any call. Another exact class string there, such as a `data-testid`, an id or an object key, is reported, as are a 0.x class in a regular expression or a longer string, a token's name in a regular expression, and a style for `env-code-subtle`. A test file is told by its path below its project's `package.json`, so a project kept in a `tests` folder is not all test files.
  - `v1-props` leaves a removed prop whose value mixes literals and an expression, such as `appearance={dark ? 'dark' : level}`, whole, and reports it.
  - `v1-app-shell` puts NavList items behind a condition, in a fragment or from a `map` into the `NavList.Root`, and reports a navigation whose items sit beside other content or that it can't see into.
  - `v1-names` reports ActionGroup, ActionCard, FeatureCard and AppShellSkeleton only where they are used other than as elements, which `v1-structure` and `v1-app-shell` rewrite.
  - Declaration files (`.d.ts`, `.d.mts`, `.d.cts`) parse as declarations, so a run no longer fails on them.

- The rename map covers the fields and choice controls: `hint` to `description`, the field variants, Select's `options` and `SelectOption`, DatePicker's `withTime`, Slider's `onValueCommitted`, DropZone's `onDropAccepted` and `onDropRejected`, RadioGroup's `orientation` and Radio's `description`, and the classes of the text field, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, Checkbox, Switch and Radio.

  `v1-props` reports each `className` and `style` on TextField, SearchField, Textarea, MarkdownEditor, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, Checkbox, Switch and Radio, which style the root element in 1.0 rather than the input, textarea or trigger, and leaves them as they are. The map's prop rules can now be report-only: a rule with a `report` and neither `to` nor `values` keeps the prop and reports every use.

- The rename map covers Dialog, Drawer, Popover, HoverCard, Toast, CommandPalette, Alert, EmptyState, Spinner and Meter: `v1-structure` turns `<Dialog placement="right">` into `<Drawer side="end">`; `v1-props` moves `tone="error"`, `size`, `width`, the CommandPalette props and the objects of `useToast().show` through the `calls` section; `v1-names` renames `ToastVariant` and `CommandPaletteSelectOptions`; `v1-classes` renames `env-modal-pane`, `env-bar`, `env-empty-copy` and the toast, spinner and empty-state modifier classes. The ESLint plugin's `no-renamed-exports` knows the renamed types.
- New transforms and map sections for the 1.0 vocabulary:
  - `v1-render` turns a literal `as` into `render`, `as="h2"` into `render={<h2 />}`, on Text, Stack and Surface, and reports an expression, and one beside a spread, which may set `as` or `render` too. Only the attribute's text changes.
  - `v1-structure` rewrites elements whose 1.0 form is another element, as the map's `structure` section lists them: ActionGroup becomes a Stack row, or a column with `fullWidth` on its labelled Buttons when stacked. An element that spreads props stays and is reported, since the spread may set what picks its form, and so does a Button that spreads props in a stacked group.
  - `v1-props` renames a prop whatever its value where the map gives it a `to`, such as Text's `tone` to `emphasis`, and moves the properties of the objects some calls take, such as `useToast().show({ … })`, as the `calls` section lists them.
  - `v1-classes` turns modifier classes into the attributes 1.0 sets, with the specificity they had, as the `classAttributes` section lists them, and reports them in markup.

  The rename map is a folder, `src/maps/v1`, of a header and one fragment per section and component family; `--map` takes such a folder or one JSON file with its sections merged.

- The rename map covers the shell, dock, editors and code:
  - WindowFrame: `TitleBar` is `WindowFrame.TitleBar` and `WindowFrame` is `WindowFrame.Root`; `v1-structure` makes a `titleBar` prop the first child and wraps the children in `WindowFrame.Content`, which takes `scrollRef` as its `ref`. `env-title-bar*` is `env-window-frame-title-bar*`.
  - PanelDock: `PanelDock` is `PanelDock.Root` and `PanelDockHeader` is `PanelDock.Header`, with their props types; `PanelDockControls` is reported as internal; `controls={null}` becomes `controls={false}`, and a `controls` element is reported. `env-panel-dock-chip*` is `env-panel-dock-tab*`. A prop rule can now match `{null}`.
  - Code: `env-code-copy-status` is `env-code-block-copy-status`.
  - Editors: RichTextEditor's `hint` is `description` and its `variant` values `outlined`, `plain` and `quiet` are `outline`, `underline` and `ghost`; `env-markdown-toolbar` and `env-markdown-editor-status` are `env-editor-toolbar` and `env-editor-status`.

  `v1-structure` lifts a prop's value into an element's children (`lift`), wraps the children in a part that takes some of the element's props (`wrapChildren`), matches a prop set to any value (`"*"` in `when`) and writes a namespace's part as an element, such as `WindowFrame.Content`.

- `v1-structure` makes an element's children from its props, as the map's `content` lists them, and limits a case to elements that set some props, whatever their values, with `has`. It rewrites 0.6's SplitView into SplitPane's parts: `start` and `end` become the two `SplitPane.Pane`s, `resizeLabel` the `label` of a `SplitPane.ResizeHandle` between them and `storageKey` `persist`, before or after `v1-names`, which maps `SplitView` to `SplitPane.Root` and `SplitViewProps` to `SplitPane.RootProps`. A SplitView that spreads props or has children is reported. `v1-classes` renames `env-split-view` to `env-split-pane`, and `env-split-view-divider` to `env-split-pane-handle`; the documentation page `split-view` is `split-pane`.
- Menus, navigation and collections take the 1.0 vocabulary, and several of their bugs are fixed.
  - Menu: `Menu.Item`'s `trailing` is `end`. An item with `href` closes the menu as it is followed and runs its `onClick`, if any, such as for analytics, and a disabled one renders no link, so nothing follows it. Every menu's popup, Menu.Root's, Menu.Submenu's, ContextMenu's and Menubar.Menu's, is the shared popup surface in the theme, scale and text direction of its trigger; Menu.RadioGroup takes `aria-labelledby`, which OrganizationSwitcher points at its heading; Menu.Root takes `side="inline-start"` and `"inline-end"`, and these parts pass `ref`, `style` and data attributes to the popup.
  - OrganizationSwitcher: `organizations`, `currentOrganizationId` and `onSelect` are `items`, `value` and `onValueChange`; `OrganizationSwitcherOrganization` is `OrganizationSwitcherItem`, whose `detail` is `description`. Its rows are Menu.RadioItems, and its strings come from `messages.organizationSwitcher`.
  - NavList.Item: `trailing` is `end`. An icon-only item, or one named by `aria-label`, says its end content in its name: a number, or a CounterBadge without `aria-label`, as a count, such as "Inbox, 12 new", from `messages.navList.itemCount`, and a CounterBadge's `aria-label` or other content's text as it is, such as "Inbox, 12 unread" or "Labs, Beta", from `messages.navList.itemEnd`; the dot alone was hidden from assistive technology. `env-nav-list-item-selected` and `-icon-only` are `[data-selected]` and `[data-icon-only]`.
  - Tabs pass the text direction to their list, so the arrow keys follow it in right-to-left text, while the panels keep the direction around them. `env-tab` and `env-tab-panel` are `env-tabs-tab` and `env-tabs-panel`.
  - DocumentTabs is rebuilt on Base UI Tabs: each tab sits beside its close button instead of around it, a CloseButton out of the tab order and hidden from assistive technology, and announces Delete and Backspace, which close it, through `aria-keyshortcuts`; each tab opens a context menu with Close, also without `onCloseAll`, which adds Close all tabs and the strip's own menu. A ContextMenu `content` function that returns `null` leaves the menu closed on that element. The arrow keys and the wheel follow the text direction; strings come from `messages.documentTabs`; the strip takes `ref` and `style`. `env-document-tab`, `-label` and `-close` are `env-document-tabs-tab`, `-tab-label` and `env-document-tabs-close`, and a new `env-document-tabs-item` holds each tab and its close button.
  - Breadcrumbs: a label cut short shows in full in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, and a plain level or the current page cut short takes keyboard focus for it; the name comes from `messages.breadcrumbs`; the `nav` takes native props. `env-breadcrumb` and `env-breadcrumb-*` are `env-breadcrumbs-item` and `env-breadcrumbs-*`.
  - Pagination formats its range in the locale and takes its strings from `messages.pagination`. Pager takes its labels from `messages.pager`, and its children render between the `previous` and `next` props' links.
  - Tree: `selectedId` and `onOpen` are `value` and `onAction`, `TreeOpenOptions` is `TreeActionOptions`, and a TreeItem's `detail` and `trailing` are `description` and `end`; it takes `ref` and `style`. `env-tree-row`, `-detail` and `-trailing` are `env-tree-item`, `-description` and `-end`, and the row height's custom property, `--env-tree-row-height`, is `--env-tree-item-height`; the `--env-tree-row-padding-block-*` tokens keep their names.
  - Table: `Table.HeaderCell`'s `onSort` is `onSortChange`, called with the order pressing asks for. A table named by `aria-labelledby` names its scroll region too. The sort and resize words come from `messages.table`.
  - List: `List.Item`'s `title` and `detail` are `label` and `description`. `List.Root`'s `contrast` is gone: a list in `<Surface background="muted" padding={3}>` of its own takes the raised row fill.
  - Kbd reads `--env-kbd-size`, `-padding-inline`, `-radius`, `-fg`, `-font-size`, `--env-kbd-separator-padding-inline` and `-font`, which a container sets to show smaller keys.

  Migrate with the codemods: `envui-codemods v1-props` renames `trailing`, OrganizationSwitcher's, Tree's, Table.HeaderCell's and List.Item's props; `v1-structure` wraps a `contrast` List.Root in a muted Surface and reports a computed one; `v1-names` renames `OrganizationSwitcherOrganization` and `TreeOpenOptions`; and `v1-classes` renames the classes, `env-menu-theme` to `env-popup-theme` among them, and turns NavList's modifiers into attributes; it reports OrganizationSwitcher's `env-organization-menu-radio` and `-radio-dot`, whose radio is Menu's `.env-menu-item-check` and `-check-mark` now, and a selector that puts `.env-document-tabs-close` inside `.env-document-tabs-tab`, its sibling now. By hand: the `detail` and `trailing` fields of Tree and OrganizationSwitcher data, which TypeScript reports, and the count in an icon-only NavList.Item's `aria-label`, which the item now adds itself. The ESLint plugin reports the renamed types.

  Migration guide: [Navigation and collections](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/migration.md#navigation-and-collections).

### Patch Changes

- `v1-classes` and `v1-render` find the text they replace by offsets in the file as it is: in a file indented with tabs, after a U+2028 or U+2029, and on the first line after a byte order mark, `v1-render` no longer writes `render` in the wrong place, and `v1-classes` no longer leaves the classes in tab-indented literals unchanged.
