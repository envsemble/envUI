# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — 2026-11-19

envUI 2.1 lets an app put what it wants where it wants. Labels across the library take any content again, components gain start, end, header and action slots and render functions, rows and items pass an app's own props through, and the lint rules report only real mistakes. Nothing breaks: every 2.0 call compiles and renders as before, and the few item types that widened say what code that reads them back needs.

### Minor Changes

- Accordion: an item takes `end`, content beside its heading and outside its button, such as a Switch, a Badge with a Menu, or a Delete IconButton. `indicator` replaces the plus and minus with the application's own, such as a chevron, or `null` for none, and `indicatorPlacement="end"` moves it after the title; set on Accordion.Root they apply to every item, and an item's own replace them. See [Accordion](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/accordion.md).
- Alert: `title` takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as a branch name in Code or a link, and `actionsPlacement="end"` puts the actions at the end of the alert's row, before the dismiss button, as in a one-line banner such as "Sync paused. Resume"; they move below the message where the alert is too narrow for both. See [Alert](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/alert.md).
- AvatarButton: `description` takes any content, such as an organization's name with its plan's Badge, and the button passes `hue`, `selected` and `badge` to its avatar, so an account trigger can take an organization's own color or show a presence dot. See [Avatar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/avatar.md).
- Avatar: `fallback` also takes an element, placed on the hue's fill in its deep shade, such as an icon or a Graphic for a bot, an AI agent, a team or a service account. A new `badge` places a small mark at the avatar's bottom end, such as a presence dot, an unread marker or an organization's mark, and moves to the top end while the avatar is selected. See [Avatar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/avatar.md).
- AvatarGroup: each person takes a `hue`, a `badge` such as a presence dot, and a `tooltip` for a pressable avatar, such as the name with a role or an e-mail address. `renderOverflowTooltip` gives the overflow chip's tooltip the application's own content, such as the hidden people with their avatars. Accessible names stay the people's names, and the pile lays out and spreads as before. See [Avatar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/avatar.md).
- Badge: the label takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as a bold value or a RelativeTime in "Expires in 3 days", and still ends with an ellipsis where it is cut short. A new `start` places content before the label, such as an icon, a small Avatar or a color swatch: an envUI icon takes the badge's icon size and other content keeps its own, and assistive technology reads it unless it hides itself, as an unlabelled icon does. A done badge leads with a check mark unless given another `start`, or `null` for none. See [Badge](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/badge.md).
- Breadcrumbs: a crumb's `label` takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as an Avatar before a project's name. It names the crumb's link or button, the trail still cuts it short beyond 20 rem, and the tooltip then shows the same content in full. The type widens from `string` to `ReactNode`: labels an app passes in keep working, and code that reads `label` back as a string needs a check that it is one. See [Breadcrumbs](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/breadcrumbs.md).
- Button: `start` places content before the label, such as an icon or a small Avatar of the person a button assigns: an envUI icon takes the button's icon size and other content keeps its own, hidden from assistive technology as the label names the button. `render` renders the button as another element, such as a label for a file input or a router's own link in one place: the button's class, look, state attributes, content and handlers merge into its props, and a disabled one reports aria-disabled and blocks presses. See [Button](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/button.md).
- Calendar: `isDateUnavailable` makes the days it returns true for unavailable, such as weekends, holidays or booked days, as days outside `min` and `max` are, and `renderDay` gives each day's mark its content in place of the number, such as the number with a dot for a day with events, or a price; each day keeps its role, its name and its keyboard handling. See [DatePicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/date-picker.md).
- Card: a content card takes `interactive`, and a link or button inside it marked `data-row-target`, such as its title, stretches over the card and takes its click, as in an interactive List row, while every other link, button and field in the card, such as an author's link in the description or a Menu in `headerEnd`, stays clickable above it. A grid of project cards that open on click can keep a title row, a status Badge and an overflow Menu. `headerEnd` puts content at the end of the title row, after the meta, such as an Edit Button or a Menu, as on Dialog and Sheet. `description` is no longer a paragraph, so a list or a row of Badges is valid there; its look is unchanged. See [Card](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/card.md).
- Checkbox: `labelPlacement="start"` puts the label before the box, such as an inline Dark mode setting whose label leads, keeping the native label that names and toggles it. See [Checkbox](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/checkbox.md).
- CodeBlock and HighlightedCodeBlock: `label` takes any content, such as a file-type icon with a path link, and the code is named by the label as shown. `headerEnd` places content at the end of the heading before the copy button (the part `env-code-block-header-end`), such as Open in editor, Run, or a switch between npm, pnpm and yarn. See [Code](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/code.md).
- CodeDiff: `headerEnd` places content at the end of the file header, such as View file, Accept or a status Badge, and `annotations` with `renderAnnotation` attach notes to lines, such as review comments. `layout="split"` sets the two versions side by side at any width, and `headingLevel` sets the level of the failed state's heading for the page's outline. See [Code](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/code.md).
- ColorPickerButton: `trigger` opens the picker from the application's own element in place of the swatch button, such as a toolbar's text color button with a bar of the color, given as an element or a function of the color's value; it takes the native props, the opening behavior and, unless it has its own, the name after the label and the color, and `Toolbar.Item` puts it in a toolbar's arrow-key path. It also takes the picker's new `swatchesFirst` and `fields`. See [ColorPicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/color-picker.md).
- ColorPicker: `fields={false}` leaves out the row of value fields, with its format select, eyedropper and copy button, and `swatchesFirst` shows the swatches above the area, for a compact picker where presets are the usual choice, such as a tag's color. See [ColorPicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/color-picker.md).
- Combobox: an option takes `end`, content at the end of its row in the list, such as a role Badge or a count, read as part of its name; its label stays the text the field shows once chosen and the default filter matches. See [Combobox](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/combobox.md).
- CommandPalette: an item takes `start`, content before its label, such as an icon or a person's Avatar among the results, where an envUI icon takes the icon size and other content keeps its own. `items` and `suggestedItems` take groups, `{ label, items }`, as Select's and Combobox's do, each under a heading that takes content, such as an icon or a count beside the group's name. `searchStart` and `searchEnd` place content in the search row, before the field and after it, such as a scope chip or a mode switch. See [CommandPalette](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/command-palette.md).
- Composer: `header` places content inside the composer above the field (the part `env-composer-header`), such as attachment chips, a quoted reply or context pills, and `end` places content in the action row just before the send button, such as a voice button, a model picker or a token count. `sendKey="mod-enter"` makes Ctrl+Enter or ⌘+Enter send and Enter start a new line, for prompts that mostly take several lines; Enter still sends by default. See [Composer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/composer.md).
- ConfirmDialog: `confirmLabel` and `cancelLabel` take content, as a Button does, such as a Kbd of the shortcut beside Delete project. See [ConfirmDialog](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/confirm-dialog.md).
- DatePicker: `popupHeader` places content at the top of the popup, above the calendar, such as presets for Tomorrow or Next Monday that set the value, and `popupFooter` content in its footer after Today and before Clear, such as a note of the time zone. It takes Calendar's `isDateUnavailable` and `renderDay` too, and Today is unavailable on a day the application makes unavailable. See [DatePicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/date-picker.md).
- Dialog: `actionsStart` puts content at the start of the actions' row, such as a destructive Delete, a Learn more link or a Don't show again checkbox; `headerStart` and `headerEnd` put content before the title and at the end of its row, such as a back button or a Menu. `closeButtonPlacement="heading"` moves the built-in close button to the heading, as an IconButton with a cross as on a Sheet, and `closeButton={false}` leaves it out, as on a Sheet, for a dialog that places its own DialogClose. `title` is optional where `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` names the dialog, such as an image viewer. The new `useDialogPending` hook gives any action component of the application's own the dialog's or sheet's pending state, where the last action isn't a plain Button. See [Dialog](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/dialog.md).
- Disclosure: `indicatorPlacement="end"` puts the chevron after the summary's words, as in a Show more link, and `panelLayout="flow"` places the details as given, so they keep their own spacing and flow, such as inline text; the stacked panel stays the default. See [Disclosure](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/disclosure.md).
- DocumentTabs: a tab's `end` holds content after its name, such as a dot for unsaved changes or a Badge of errors, left out of the tab's name. A tab whose `closable` is false has no close button and stays open on a middle click, Delete and Backspace, such as a pinned or welcome tab, and its context menu leaves out Close. The new `contextMenu` adds the application's own items, such as Close others or Copy path, after the built-in ones, for the tab people open the menu on or the strip around the tabs. See [DocumentTabs](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/document-tabs.md).
- DropZone: `children` places content inside the zone, under its target, such as the chosen files with their previews and Remove buttons, or a Browse button that opens the file picker through `inputRef`. Files dropped on it are taken too, and it can hold controls, since it sits beside the target button rather than inside it. See [DropZone](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/drop-zone.md).
- EmptyState: `illustration` shows a decorative drawing or image above the title at its own size, no wider than the region, in place of `icon`, which takes the icon size. See [EmptyState](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/empty-state.md).
- Field: `Field.Error` takes `render`, the element or function to render the message as, which was dropped before, and `icon`, another icon before the message or null for none, such as for a compact error beside a control. See [Field](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/field.md).
- HoverCard: `underline={false}` leaves a text trigger without the dotted underline, `env-hover-detail`, for text styled its own way, such as @mentions in a feed. See [HoverCard](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/hover-card.md).
- Kbd: each of `keys` takes any content, such as an icon for an arrow or Return, and `separator` sets what joins them: a + by default, `null` for none as in ⌘⇧P, or a word for a sequence such as G then I. See [Kbd](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/kbd.md).
- List: a titled List.Root takes `headerEnd`, content at the end of the title's line and outside the heading (the part `env-list-header-end`), such as an Add button, a Manage link or a count. In an interactive List.Item, links, buttons and fields in the label, the description or the children, such as an author's link, now stay clickable above the stretched `data-row-target`, as in Table. `handlePlacement="end"` puts a reorderable list's drag handles at the end of each row. See [List](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/list.md).
- Markdown: `allowHtml` keeps raw HTML, such as details, sub, sup or an img with a width, for trusted content such as an application's own docs or a README. Only a rehype-raw in `rehypePlugins` renders it, so give a sanitizer such as rehype-sanitize with it; raw HTML is still dropped by default. See [Markdown](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/markdown.md).
- Menu: `Menu.Item`, `Menu.CheckboxItem`, `Menu.RadioItem` and `Menu.Submenu` take `start`, content before the label, such as an icon or an Avatar in an account switcher: an envUI icon takes the icon size and other content keeps its own, the menu's leading column widens only when some row's start holds more than an icon, and the labels of every row still line up. The items take the native attributes of their row too, such as `style`, `id`, `ref` and data attributes for a test id or analytics, and `Menu.Item` takes `closeOnClick={false}` to keep the menu open after an action people repeat, such as Zoom in. Typing in an open menu matches each item's label, not the text of its `start` content, and `textValue` gives it the label's text where the label isn't a string. `Menu.Submenu` takes `end`, such as a count beside its chevron, and `triggerProps`, the className, style, ref and attributes of the row that opens it, typed `Menu.TriggerProps`. See [Menu](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/menu.md).
- Menubar: `Menubar.Menu` takes `triggerProps`, the className, style, ref and attributes of its trigger button, typed `Menu.TriggerProps` as a `Menu.Submenu`'s are, and its `accessKey` is underlined in a label that holds an icon beside its text or wraps it in an element, such as a span, too. See [Menubar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/menubar.md).
- Message: `author` takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as a link to a profile with a Bot or Owner Badge, and `statusLabel` takes content such as a sentence with a Try again link. A new `start` places content in a column beside the message, such as the author's Avatar or an assistant's mark. `status="working"` shows a reply still being written, with a spinner and Working or its `statusLabel`, such as the step an agent is on, and the reply streamed so far: render it last in a MessageList with `aria-busy`, so the thinking row lines up with the other messages, its avatar in `start` and a Stop button in `actions` that assistive technology reaches. See [Message](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/message.md).
- Meter: `layout="inline"` puts the label, the bar and the value on one line, as in a table's usage column or a list row; stacked stays the default. See [Meter](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/meter.md).
- MonthPicker: `popupHeader` places content at the top of the popup, above its months, such as presets that set the value, and `popupFooter` content in its footer after This month and before Clear, such as a note of the fiscal year. See [MonthPicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/month-picker.md).
- NavigationBar: `visibleLabel={false}` shows each destination's label only in a tooltip, as NavigationRail's does, and a destination's new `textValue`, `itemProps`, `disabled` and `count` of true work in the bar and in its More menu. See [NavigationDrawer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/navigation-drawer.md).
- NavigationDrawer: `groupEnd` puts content after a group's heading, outside it, such as an IconButton that adds a project to Projects or a Menu of the group's actions. A destination's new `textValue`, `itemProps`, `disabled` and `count` of true work in the drawer too, and a section's button takes its name from `textValue` where the label is content. See [NavigationDrawer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/navigation-drawer.md).
- NavigationRail: `visibleLabel={false}` makes an icon-only rail, such as a code editor's activity bar: each label shows only in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus, and still names its destination. A destination's `label` also takes content, such as a name with a small Beta mark, with its text in `textValue`; `itemProps` sets more props on its link or button, such as `target`, `rel` or data attributes; `disabled` dims it and takes it out of reach; and a `count` of true shows a dot with no number, named as Activity, new, from the new `messages.navigation.itemNew`. These destination fields work in NavigationBar and NavigationDrawer too. The type of a destination's `label` widens from `string` to `ReactNode`, and its `count` from `number` to `number | true`: destinations an app passes in keep working, and code that reads `label` back as a string, or `count` as a number, needs a check that it is one. See [NavigationDrawer](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/navigation-drawer.md).
- PageHeader renders its `children` under the toolbar, placed as given, such as a progress strip or an inline Alert, and they stick with the header. Before, children type-checked but were dropped. See [Page](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/page.md).
- Pager: `Pager.Previous` and `Pager.Next` take `label`, the direction label above the title for that link alone, such as Previous chapter or Back to setup, in place of `messages.pager.previous` or `next`; `null` leaves it out. See [Pager](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/pager.md).
- Pagination: `pageHref` gives each page its address, such as `?page=3`, and the previous and next controls are then links through the LinkProvider, which open in a new tab, are crawlable and work before hydration; a control with no page to go to reports aria-disabled and has no destination. `onPageChange` is optional with `pageHref`. See [Pagination](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/pagination.md).
- Panes: a pane an application renders alone, such as the list while no item is open, fills the region whichever pane is sized, and the resize handle hides. Before, it kept the sized width or sat in the last column. See [Panes](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/panes.md).
- Popover: `trigger` is optional for a popover the application opens with `open`, and `anchor` opens it beside an element, its ref or a virtual element with a getBoundingClientRect, such as a table cell or a range of selected text. See [Popover](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/popover.md).
- Progress: `label` is optional, for a bare bar named with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, such as by the file name beside it in a row; without a label or `valueText` the bar has no row above it. See [Progress](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/progress.md).
- Radio: `label` is optional. A Radio without one is named and described by the List.Item whose `control` it is, as a Checkbox and a Switch are, so a plan picker's rows can carry a price and a Badge, or by its `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, such as a radio in a table's cell; its RadioGroup still gives it the shared name and state. See [Radio](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/radio.md).
- RelativeTime: `renderTooltip` gives the tooltip the application's own content from the moment, such as the time with its time zone, in UTC, or "Edited by Alex Morgan at 9:41". See [RelativeTime](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/relative-time.md).
- Scaffold takes `aside`, a region at the end edge beside the body, such as an inspector, and `bottomBar`, a bar along the bottom, such as a status bar. Both stay in view while the document scrolls, as the rail and the top bar do: the bottom bar sits above a NavigationBar, and focus scrolls clear of it. Without them the layout is unchanged. See [Scaffold](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/scaffold.md).
- SearchField: `endAction` places a control at the field's end after the clear button, such as a Filters IconButton, or a quiet hint such as a shortcut's Kbd, and the query stops short of both. `label` takes any content, as TextField's does. See [TextField](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/text-field.md).
- SegmentedControl: an item's `label` takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as a status dot beside the text; a full-width control still cuts it short and its tooltip shows the same content whole. An item takes `end`, content after the label and count such as a Badge, read as part of its name, and `itemProps`, more props for its button, such as a title or data attributes for tests, its class beside the segment's own. With `visibleLabel: false` a segment shows only its `start`, such as an icon, as in a List and Grid view switch: its label still names it and shows in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus. The type of an item's `label` widens from `string` to `ReactNode`: items an app passes in keep working, and code that reads `label` back as a string needs a check that it is one. See [SegmentedControl](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/segmented-control.md).
- Select: an option takes `start` content before its label, such as a status dot, a priority's icon, a flag or an Avatar, which the trigger also shows before the chosen label, and `end` content in the list, such as a count or a Badge. `items` also takes groups, `{ label, items }`, each under a heading that names it for assistive technology. The label stays the text people type ahead on and the trigger shows. The type of `items` widens from `readonly SelectItem[]` to `readonly SelectItem[] | readonly SelectGroup[]`: lists an app passes in keep working, and code that reads `items` back as options needs a check that an entry isn't a group. See [Select](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/select.md).
- Sheet: `actionsStart` puts content at the start of the actions' row, such as a Reset button, and `headerStart` and `headerEnd` put content before the title and before the close button, such as a back button, a Menu of more actions or an Open full page button. See [Sheet](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/sheet.md).
- Slider: `label` takes any content again, as in 1.x, such as a volume icon beside the text; it still names the slider and ends in an ellipsis where the slider is narrow. `renderValue` shows the value at the slider's end as any content, such as the number with its unit styled apart, while `formatValue`, or the number in the locale, stays what assistive technology announces. The type of `label` widens from `string` to `ReactNode`: code that reads it back as a string needs a check that it is one. See [Slider](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/slider.md).
- Switch: `labelPlacement="start"` puts the label before the track, such as an inline Dark mode setting whose label leads, keeping the native label that names and toggles it. See [Switch](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/switch.md).
- Table: `Table.Foot` renders a tfoot for totals or a summary row, on the header's shaded band, keeping its roles while rows stack. Table.HeaderCell takes `end`, content beside the sort button and outside it, such as a column menu, a filter IconButton or a select-all Checkbox; the header's name stays its label. Table.Root takes `regionProps` for the scroll region, such as a ref for scrolling to a row, a style with a measured height, data attributes or listeners. See [Table](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/table.md).
- TextField and SearchField: `prefix` and `suffix` take any content again, as in 1.x, such as a currency's icon beside its code or a small Avatar before the value, at its own size; the field still cuts them short with an ellipsis, and reads their text after the label. The type widens from `string` to `ReactNode`: code that reads them back as strings needs a check that they are. See [TextField](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/text-field.md).
- ThemeMenuRadioGroup takes `children`, Menu.RadioItems valued system, light or dark, in place of its three default choices, such as Light and Dark alone or a choice with a hint beside it, and passes `className` to the group. Its `closeOnClick` applies to the given choices too, unless one sets its own. See [Menu](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/menu.md).
- Theme takes `render`, like Box and Text: its boundary can be the element a region needs, such as `render={<section />}` for a landmark or `render={<tbody />}` inside a table, with the boundary's class and attributes merged into it as on Box and Text, its styles, event handlers and refs kept beside the boundary's, instead of an extra div. See [Setup](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/setup.md#theme).
- ThemeToggle takes `icon`, such as one of the application's own, in place of the dark and light mode icons; its name stays Switch to dark theme or Switch to light theme. See [Setup](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/setup.md#theme-preference).
- TimePicker: `popupHeader` places content at the top of the popup, above its list, such as presets that set the value, and `popupFooter` content in its footer after its shortcut to the current time and before Clear, such as a note of the time zone. See [TimePicker](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/time-picker.md).
- ToastProvider: a notification's `message` takes content, such as the name of what changed in bold or a link to it, and its native title and announcement take its text: `textValue`, else the message when it is a string, else the message's rendered text. `icon` replaces the tone's icon, such as with an Avatar for a mention, or `null` shows none. An action takes `href`, which makes it a link through the LinkProvider, with an optional `onClick`; an action without `href` needs its `onClick`. The type of a notification's `message` widens from `string` to `ReactNode`, and an action's `onClick` is optional on a link: code that reads `message` back as a string, or calls an action's `onClick`, needs a check that it is one, or that it is there. See [Toast](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/toast.md).
- Toolbar: `Toolbar.Item` makes any button-like control one stop of the toolbar's arrow-key path, such as a Button with a visible label, `render={<Button start={<IconShare />}>Share</Button>}`, or a Menu's trigger, `trigger={<Toolbar.Item render={<Button>Paragraph</Button>} />}`. `Toolbar.Input` does the same for a text input, such as a font size. See [Toolbar](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/toolbar.md).
- Tooltip: `underline={false}` leaves a text trigger without the dotted underline, `env-hover-detail`, as on a HoverCard. See [Tooltip](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/tooltip.md).
- Tree: a row's `label` and `description` take any content again, as in 1.x, such as a name with the letters a search matched in bold, and type-ahead matches the label's rendered text, or `textValue` where that isn't the name. A row's `itemProps` sets its own props on the row element, such as a className or data attribute for its state, or `draggable` and drag handlers for moving files; the tree keeps its role, id, ARIA attributes and title. The tree also takes `onClick`, `onDoubleClick`, `onContextMenu` and `onPointerDown`, which run before its own handling; a click or double click whose default is prevented doesn't open the row. The types of `label` and `description` widen from `string` to `ReactNode`: rows an app passes in keep working, and code that reads `label` or `description` back as a string needs a check that it is one. See [Tree](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/tree.md).

### Patch Changes

- AvatarButton and a pressable AvatarGroup avatar: a `badge` given as text, such as online, now follows the name in the button's or link's accessible name, as in Alex Morgan, online, so the name includes the text the button shows. A badge that is an element, such as a presence dot, leaves the name as it was.
- Pager: where the previous and next links don't fit side by side, such as on a phone, they stack as rows of the pager's full width, the previous first, rather than wrap into two centred cards of different widths.
- Toolbar: a separator stays visible on the recessed surface in the light theme, where it had faded to almost the well's own shade since 2.0's deeper well. In light it is now a tint of the text color, so it keeps the same step from any surface a toolbar sits on; dark and high contrast look as before.
- Breadcrumbs and TableOfContents: on a touch screen, a breadcrumb's link has a touch target of 2.75 rem (44 px) around it, and a wrapping trail's lines sit far enough apart for it; each TableOfContents link is 2.75 rem tall. With a mouse both look as before.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-19

envUI 2.0 steers by its code and looks quieter. Fewer, better components: Tabs, LineArt and PanelDock are gone, Panes is one part with a resize handle, and EmptyState, ResizeHandle and VisuallyHidden arrive. The light and dark themes keep things apart, and alike: light surfaces sit a step apart around a real recessed well, selection stays apart from hover, and nothing shows in one theme and disappears in the other. Each breaking change below says what to write instead.

### Major Changes

- **envUI 2.0.** This release removes and renames parts of the 1.x API, among them Tabs, LineArt, PanelDock and Panes' supporting pane. Each change in it lists what it breaks under its own "Breaking changes:" line, with what to write instead.

### Minor Changes

- Buttons and fields share one shape, and their default look is quieter:

  - **One corner for every control.** The new token `--env-radius-control` is the corner of every button, in each variant, of every text field, of the triggers of Select, Combobox and the pickers, of a Slider, and of a SegmentedControl's track. It follows the theme's `corners` setting, so `corners="sharp"` squares buttons and fields alike. Buttons are no longer pills, and icon buttons are rounded squares.
  - **Secondary buttons are a quiet fill.** A secondary button, and an IconButton without a variant, takes the translucent neutral fill, `--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`, with no edge, and its hover and pressed grades under the pointer and while pressed; an outline button keeps the thin edge. Its focus ring lies on its edge, as an outline button's does. It still joins a primary button in a ButtonGroup.
  - **A pressed toggle is gray.** Any Button or IconButton with `aria-pressed="true"` takes the selection fill, `--env-color-bg-selected`, which is neutral unless the theme's `selection` is `'accent'`, with `--env-button-selected` and `--env-button-selected-content` as its color properties. Toolbar.Button's toggles take it from Button now.
  - **Fields show their label above the box.** TextField, Textarea, SearchField, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, TimePicker and MonthPicker put their label above the field, as Field does, in the label size and the muted color. A small field keeps the small height while its label shows, and a prefix or suffix always shows.
  - **Primary buttons are solid in dark.** A primary button takes `--env-color-bg-primary-strong` and its hover and pressed tokens, under `--env-color-fg-on-strong`, a white label, in every theme. The dark emphasis fill, which checked checkboxes, switches that are on, radios, meters and spinners take with dark marks, is a stronger blue than before.

  - Panes' `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `closeLabel` and `backLabel` are gone, with the types `OpenChangeReason` and `OpenChangeDetails` and the messages `panes.back` and `panes.close`; an application keeps whether a pane shows and renders it or not.
  - `Panes.Main`, `Panes.Supporting`, `Panes.List` and `Panes.Detail` are one part, `Panes.Pane`, with `label` and `header`; Panes.Supporting's `closeButton` and `skeleton` are gone. `Panes.Trigger` is gone. `Panes.Divider` is `Panes.ResizeHandle`, with the same `label`.
  - The parts `env-panes-main`, `env-panes-supporting`, `env-panes-list`, `env-panes-detail`, `env-panes-divider`, `env-panes-back`, `env-panes-close`, `env-panes-backdrop` and `env-panes-trigger` are gone: a pane is `env-panes-pane` and the handle `env-panes-resize-handle`. The states `data-behavior` (`data-behavior='supporting'`, `data-behavior='list-detail'`), `data-split-at` (`data-split-at='medium'`, `data-split-at='expanded'`, `data-split-at='large'`), `data-open`, `data-closed` and `data-sheet-only` are gone; the root takes `data-sized` and `data-stack-below`.
  - The resize handle is invisible but for its grip, with no line at rest, and its grip keeps its color with keyboard focus, where a line in the focus color shows instead of an outline around the handle.
  - The token `--env-z-supporting-pane` is gone with the supporting pane's sheet: a layer of the application's own over the page takes `--env-z-sticky` plus one, or `--env-z-modal` for a modal one.

- Pagination and Pager are quiet neutral controls. Pagination's previous and next buttons are ghost icon buttons by default, with no fill until hovered; `variant` still takes another. A Pager link has no box or edge and a muted arrow, and takes the neutral hover and pressed fill of a quiet button with the control corners.

  Breaking changes:

  - Pagination's `variant` defaults to `ghost` rather than `secondary`: write `variant="secondary"` for the filled buttons.
  - A Pager link (`env-pager-link`) no longer draws a border on the raised surface or the primary color on its arrow; give its class a border and background for the earlier look.

- Components that fill a region of the screen fill it edge to edge with square corners by default, and an application still rounds or spaces one with its own class, as any element: `.inbox { border-radius: var(--env-radius-lg); }` rounds Panes, and the panes inside follow its corners, since the root clips them. DocumentTabs' root clips to the radius it is given in the same way.

  Breaking changes:

  - **Panes sit side by side with no gap**: the panes touch, rather than 1 rem of the page apart; an application that wants them apart sets a margin or a gap on them.

- LineArt is gone: envUI no longer ships decorative drawings or their motion.

  Breaking changes:

  - `LineArt`, `lineArtDrawings` and the types `LineArtProps`, `LineArtDrawing` and `LineArtMotion` are removed, with the part `env-line-art`, its property `--env-line-art-duration` and its states `data-align` (`data-align='center'`, `data-align='end'`, `data-align='start'`); `data-drawing` (`data-drawing='ford-circles'`, `data-drawing='golden-section'`, `data-drawing='gothic-tracery'`, `data-drawing='graded-quadtree'`, `data-drawing='harmonic-division'`, `data-drawing='logarithmic-grid'`, `data-drawing='monochord'`, `data-drawing='pappus-chain'`, `data-drawing='pointed-arcade'`, `data-drawing='quartered-square'`, `data-drawing='root-rectangles'`, `data-drawing='root-two-halving'`, `data-drawing='sacred-cut'`, `data-drawing='square-circle-triangle'`, `data-drawing='triangle-and-circles'`, `data-drawing='whirling-squares'`); `data-motion` (`data-motion='appear'`, `data-motion='disappear'`, `data-motion='loop'`, `data-motion='play'`); `data-turn` (`data-turn='in'`, `data-turn='out'`). Draw an illustration of the application's own as an SVG in a Graphic instead.

- PanelDock is gone: docking, minimizing and maximizing panels is an application's own logic. A panel people keep open beside the page is a Sheet, or a pane of Panes.

  Breaking changes:

  - **PanelDock is removed**: `PanelDock.Root`, `PanelDock.Header` and their props, the types `PanelDockMode` and `PanelDockPanel`, the messages `panelDock` (`minimize`, `maximize`, `restore`, `closeWindow`, `minimized`, `open`, `close` and `resize`), its parts `env-panel-dock`, `env-panel-dock-row`, `env-panel-dock-panel`, `env-panel-dock-header`, `env-panel-dock-heading`, `env-panel-dock-title`, `env-panel-dock-meta`, `env-panel-dock-header-content`, `env-panel-dock-actions`, `env-panel-dock-controls`, `env-panel-dock-content`, `env-panel-dock-resize-handle`, `env-panel-dock-strip`, `env-panel-dock-tab`, `env-panel-dock-tab-label`, `env-panel-dock-tab-attention`, `env-panel-dock-tab-restore`, `env-panel-dock-tab-close` and `env-panel-dock-probe`, and its states `data-active` (`data-active=''`), `data-mode` (`data-mode='open'`, `data-mode='minimized'`, `data-mode='maximized'`), `data-placement` (`data-placement='row'`, `data-placement='fill'`, `data-placement='hidden'`), `data-panel-id`, `data-entering`, `data-attention` (`data-attention=''`), `data-filled` (`data-filled=''`), `data-overflowing` (`data-overflowing=''`), `data-resizing` (`data-resizing=''`), `data-scrolled` (`data-scrolled=''`), `data-stacked` (`data-stacked=''`) and `data-tabs` (`data-tabs=''`). Write the panels as the application's own, with a [Sheet](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/sheet.md) for one beside the page or [Panes](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/panes.md) for one in the layout.
  - A Scaffold's body no longer places a PanelDock across its scrollport.

- Tabs is gone: views of the same content switch with a [SegmentedControl](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/components/segmented-control.md), and places to go are a NavigationDrawer or a NavigationRail. MarkdownEditor's Write and Preview are a small SegmentedControl above the source, in the new parts `env-markdown-editor-views` and `env-markdown-editor-view`. ESLint's `envui/no-html-elements` suggests SegmentedControl for an element with the role `tablist`, or DocumentTabs for documents people open and close.

  Breaking changes:

  - **Tabs is removed**, with `TabsProps` and `TabsItem`, its parts `env-tabs`, `env-tabs-list`, `env-tabs-tab`, `env-tabs-indicator`, `env-tabs-panels` and `env-tabs-panel`, its states `data-orientation` (`data-orientation='vertical'`), `data-active`, `data-placed`, `data-starting-style` and `data-ending-style`, `data-variant` (`data-variant='text'`), `data-size` (`data-size='sm'`, `data-size='md'`, `data-size='lg'`) and `data-fill` (`data-fill=''`, with `data-placed=''`), and its color properties `--env-tabs-container`, `--env-tabs-content`, `--env-tabs-selected-content` and `--env-tabs-indicator`. Write a SegmentedControl for the views and show the chosen one's content under it: `<SegmentedControl aria-label="Project views" value={view} onValueChange={setView} items={[{ value: 'summary', label: 'Summary' }, { value: 'activity', label: 'Activity' }]} />`, then `{view === 'summary' ? <Summary /> : <Activity />}`. A side list of sections is a NavigationDrawer.
  - MarkdownEditor's Write and Preview are radios in a SegmentedControl rather than tabs, so a test that finds them by the role `tab` finds them by `radio`, and the source and the preview are no longer `tabpanel`s.

- Rows of switches and checks name their control, and the rows around them look alike:

  - **A row names its control.** `List.Item` takes `control`: the row's Switch or Checkbox, at its end. Given no label of its own, the control takes its accessible name from the row's `label` and its description from the row's `description`, so a row needs no hand-wired ids and isn't read twice. A control with its own label keeps it. A field, such as a Select, a TextField or a RadioGroup, belongs in a form with its label above it; the new ESLint rule `envui/no-field-in-list-row` reports one in a List.Item's `control` or `end`.
  - **A titled group of rows.** `List.Root` takes `title`, a heading above the rows that names the list, `headingLevel` (2 by default) and `description`, a muted sentence under the title that describes it. New parts: `env-list-section`, `env-list-header`, `env-list-title`, `env-list-description` and the slot `env-list-item-control`.
  - **envUI's icons read `--env-icon-size`.** Every symbol icon is `--env-icon-size` square (the new part `env-icon-symbol`), so an icon in a `List.Item`'s `start` is the large icon size and centred on the row again, an icon in TopBar's and PageHeader's `start` the picture size, and elsewhere the control icon size rather than 1 em.
  - **Fields that apply at once commit.** TextField and SearchField take `onValueCommitted`, called with the value on Enter and when the field loses focus with a value that changed; with it, Esc puts back the value last committed. Return `false` from it to reject the value, such as a port out of range: the value stays in the field to correct, and Esc still puts back the last value accepted.
  - **Secondary and outline are told apart.** A secondary button is a quiet neutral fill, `--env-color-bg-neutral-muted`, with the control corners and no edge; an outline button keeps the thin edge.
  - **A white page in light.** `--env-color-bg-page` is white in the light theme rather than a mid gray, so the frame and the content on it read crisp, with a hairline between them. The surfaces on it are tinted a step deeper: `--env-color-bg-default` (panes, sheets, Surface) gray 2, `--env-color-bg-muted` (cards and filled rows) gray 4, `--env-color-bg-recessed` gray 6, and `--env-color-bg-field` the recessed gray 6, each with its hover and pressed grades a step deeper; `--env-color-bg-raised` and `--env-color-bg-popup` stay white and part from the page by their edge. Dark and high contrast are unchanged. An application that set `--env-color-bg-page` to white or a light gray can drop that.
  - **A lighter neutral selection.** `--env-color-bg-selected` and its hover and pressed grades are a step lighter in light (gray 11, 8 and 7) and in dark (gray 75, 80 and 84); a selected item keeps its second cue, its weight, bar or check.
  - The guidance of Select, RadioGroup, SegmentedControl and NavigationDrawer no longer sends a setting in circles: a setting with a few options is a Select in a form, RadioGroup is for options that need a sentence or must all stay in view, and SegmentedControl for views and filters.

  Breaking changes:

  - **RadioGroup lists its options one under the other by default**: `orientation` defaults to `vertical`, which no longer draws selectable rows; write `orientation="horizontal"` for options side by side. Each option is a radio before its label, with its description under the label: a 1.25 rem ring of `--env-color-border-default`, the primary color with a dot when chosen, in a 2.5 rem circle, the new part `env-radio-control`, that shows hover, focus and press as a state layer; the label keeps the text color and pressing it chooses the option. The rows' fill, tiles and corners are gone, with the color property `--env-radio-container`, and RadioGroup and Radio no longer take the `env-connected-rows` and `env-connected-row` classes, so those parts no longer have `data-orientation` (`data-orientation='horizontal'`, `data-orientation='vertical'`). `--env-radio-content` colors the label, and `--env-radio-selected` the chosen ring, dot and state layer. Radios don't go in list rows: for rows people switch on and off, put a Switch or Checkbox in each List.Item's `control`.
  - An envUI icon placed on its own is sized by `--env-icon-size` rather than 1 em of the text around it. To size one icon, set `--env-icon-size` on it or on its container.

- The guides are short, and the customizing and foundations pages are generated:

  - **Customize** (`docs/customizing.md`) is the styling contract of every component, generated from `docs/generated/parts.json`: its part classes, with the slots marked, its state attributes, its color properties and its public properties, beside the theme settings (`surface`, `selection`, `corners` in `defineTheme`), recoloring an area through the tokens, and the tokens layouts line up with.
  - **The foundations** (`docs/foundations/`) are their figures and a few sentences, with every token in a generated table: colors by role in light and dark, spacing, corners, controls and rows, type, icon sizes, window classes, layout widths, the stacking order and motion, each with its value in both scales and what it is for.
  - **Setup, Writing and Dev mode** keep what installs and runs with a few sentences around it. Writing keeps its vocabulary table, which `envui/label-vocabulary` reads, and a few lines on sentence case and the ellipsis on an action that asks for more.

- Themes take two settings for their look besides color and scale, on `Theme`, in the theme `ThemePreferenceProvider` takes, and from `useTheme()`. Each boundary writes them as `data-env-selection` and `data-env-corners`, nested Themes and popups keep them, and they work through the tokens, so every component follows:

  - `selection`: `'neutral'`, the default, or `'accent'`. **The selection is neutral by default now**: `--env-color-bg-selected`, `-hover` and `-pressed` are a gray deeper than the surfaces in every theme and contrast, and `--env-color-fg-on-selected` is the default text color. The blue selection is `selection="accent"`, and its values are the new tokens `--env-color-bg-selected-accent`, `-accent-hover`, `-accent-pressed` and `--env-color-fg-on-selected-accent`. An application that overrode the four selection tokens to make them neutral can drop that.
  - `corners`: `'sharp'`, `'rounded'`, the default, or `'round'`. Sharp corners are near square, `--env-radius-full` included; round ones are larger. Parts that are always circles stay circles.

  `--env-icon-size` is a public token again, the size of the icons in a place: 1 rem at the root, and a component that sizes its icons, such as a button or a navigation item, sets it on its own element, so content can line up with it.

  A Badge, a count or a DiffStat inside a Button keeps its own colors: the button no longer recolors its content.

  Breaking changes:

  - **One theme definition.** `defineTheme({ storageKey, defaultPreference, contrast, scale, selection, corners })` defines an application's theme once, and both `<ThemePreferenceProvider theme={theme}>` and `createThemeBootstrapScript(theme)` take it, so the first paint and the application can't drift apart. ThemePreferenceProvider's `storageKey`, `defaultPreference`, `contrast` and `scale` props are gone: write `<ThemePreferenceProvider theme={defineTheme({ storageKey: 'app-theme', scale: 'compact' })}>` instead of `<ThemePreferenceProvider storageKey="app-theme" scale="compact">`. The bootstrap script now also writes the selection and corners to the root before the first paint.
  - `ThemeBootstrapOptions` is renamed `ThemeDefinition`.
  - The selection tokens are neutral by default, as above; write `selection: 'accent'` for the blue selection.
  - The button's label, icons and end are each wrapped in a part of their own, `env-button-label`, `env-button-start-icon`, `env-button-end-icon` and `env-button-end`, which only place what they hold. A label is one line of text in its own part, so a badge in it sits in the line, a word space after the text, rather than the button's gap away.

  See [Setup](https://github.com/envsemble/envUI/blob/main/docs/setup.md#theme-preference).

### Patch Changes

- SegmentedControl's thumb travels only to a newly chosen segment: as the control resizes, such as a full-width control in a container that narrows, it stays over the chosen segment instead of easing after it, and the thumb marks a move with `data-traveling`.
- Slider's ticks keep one color while dragging: the fill leaves a gap under each tick while they show, so a tick over the fill looks as it does over the track, rather than lighter where the fill reaches it. The ticks are drawn from the track's start edge, in both directions.
- **The light and dark themes keep things apart, and alike.** Colors take other grades of the same roles, so apps that use the tokens follow without changes.

  - Light surfaces sit a step apart: the default and muted surfaces are a grade or two deeper, fields a grade deeper, and the recessed surface four grades deeper (`#dadadc`), a well that cards and list rows stand out in as they do in dark's. `Surface background="raised"` takes the default surface's tint in light, so a raised panel reads as its own background on the white page, as it does in dark.
  - The dark page is a grade darker, with the recessed surface and fields below it, and the muted surface a grade lighter, so panes, cards and navigation stand off the page.
  - Raised and recessed Surfaces take a faint 1 px frosted edge in both themes, which makes one sized by its content 2 px larger; in light, popups, dialogs, the command palette, rich text suggestions and tooltips take the same edge that dark popups had.
  - Selection stays apart from hover, press and the list cursor: light selection is two grades deeper, and a selected item under the pointer or pressed a grade deeper still, so it never lands on a surface's gray; popup rows give the cursor a quieter fill than the selection; light navigation hover and press are translucent layers; dark table rows hover below a full-strength selection.
  - The light primary color is two grades deeper at rest, hover and press, the light focus ring a grade deeper, and light primary, danger, success, accent and done text, the default border and the popup and dialog edges a grade deeper, so they keep their contrast on the deeper recessed surface; dark primary and danger fills press two grades from rest.
  - State steps show in both themes: field hover borders, ghost fields from read-only to hover, segments, menubar triggers, toolbar toggles, cards, radios, drop zones, tree rows, sortable table headers, resize grips and the color picker button.
  - Disabled states stay readable: checkbox and radio labels take the disabled text color (GrayText under forced colors), a disabled chosen radio its gray, a disabled pressed toggle a faded fill, a disabled chosen segment its fill with the label in the disabled text color, and a disabled card's description a little more contrast. An invalid Slider takes the danger edge invalid fields have.
  - Tracks, slider fills and ticks, the AvatarGroup overflow chip, soft neutral and done badges, the contents wave, dark tooltips, dark alerts, the heatmap's lowest levels and a failed message's edge are firmer, and soft badges in a selected table row keep the table's ground; out-of-range calendar dates are dimmed once instead of twice; a field's prefix and suffix take the full muted text color.
  - A solid count badge takes its tone's text color, so a dark danger count is white like the danger badge.
